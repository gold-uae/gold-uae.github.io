About Gold UAE

Gold UAE is an independent website about gold in the United Arab Emirates, written in English and Arabic for the people who actually buy, sell, wear and save gold here: residents on a monthly salary, families planning a wedding, tourists with a VAT refund form, and investors weighing a bar against a fund. We publish live rates, practical tools and plainly written guides. We do not sell gold, and no shop or brand pays to be mentioned.

What the site covers

The site is organised around what a reader wants to do rather than around topics. Each section answers one kind of question.

  • Rates: the live per-gram rate for 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K, the same across all seven emirates, plus the dollar spot price and conversions into rupees and other home currencies. Start at the rates hub.
  • Karats and purity: what 999, 916, 875 and 750 mean, hallmarks, and how to spot fake gold. See the karat guide.
  • Tools: a gold calculator, live chart and price history.
  • Buying and selling: making charges, VAT, where to buy, how to sell, gold loans and customs limits for India.
  • Souks and jewellery: Deira, Gold and Diamond Park, Sharjah, Ajman and the pieces people actually shop for.
  • Investing: bars, coins, online trading, ETFs and price analysis without predictions.

How the numbers are produced

The rates on this site are fetched in your browser from public price feeds for the international gold spot price in US dollars and for currency exchange rates. The AED gram price for each karat is then calculated from the troy ounce weight, the fixed dirham-to-dollar peg and the purity of the karat. This mirrors how the Dubai retail rate is built, but it is a calculation, not the official board of any shop or trade body. Retail boards include the local trade's margin and are updated at their own pace, so a small difference between our figure and a shop window is normal.

Every article is written from scratch by people who know the UAE gold trade, in both languages separately; the Arabic pages are not machine translations of the English ones. We do not quote dated prices in text, because a number written today is wrong tomorrow. Where a fact can change, such as customs duty or VAT refund rules, we tell you where to check the current version.

What we are not

We are not a jeweller, a broker, a bank or a financial adviser. We do not buy or sell gold, we do not value your jewellery from a photo, and we do not publish forecasts. Guides use phrases like "many investors" and "historically" on purpose: they describe what people in the market do, not what you should do. Before you act on anything here, especially with a large amount, speak to a licensed professional and read our disclaimer.

If you spot a mistake, a stale fact or a souk that has moved, we want to hear about it. The contact page explains how to reach us and what we can help with.
