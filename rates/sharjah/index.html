Gold Rate in Sharjah Today: Live Price per Gram and the Rolla Gold Rows

Sharjah is the emirate where value hunters shop. The gold rate in Sharjah is exactly the figure you see on the live board below, the same national per-gram price that Dubai and Abu Dhabi use, converted from the dollar spot price at the fixed peg. What draws buyers across the Al Nahda and Al Mamzar borders is not a cheaper gram but a cluster of old-fashioned, high-turnover jewellers who compete hard on making charges.

This page gives you the live rate for 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K, then takes you street by street through the Rolla gold rows, the Central Souk and the malls, with the habits that get Sharjah residents the best price.

  • Sharjah's 22 carat rate is 91.6% of the 24K line, the same proportion as in Dubai.

Jewellers in Sharjah tend to update their physical boards a little less often than the big Dubai chains, so on a jumpy day the number in the window can sit above or below the live figure for a few hours. That is not a trick; most owners set the board at opening and after lunch. If you are buying a heavy piece, ask what rate the invoice will use and whether it is today's morning fix or the live price.

The arithmetic is the same here as everywhere in the country: dollar ounce price divided by 31.1035, multiplied by the dirham peg, then scaled by purity for each karat. For a deeper look at the 22-carat line, the 22K gold rate page carries a dedicated card; the who sets the rate page explains why Sharjah uses a figure published in Dubai.

Rolla and Al Shuwaihean: where Sharjah buys by weight

Ask a Sharjah resident where to buy gold and the first word is Rolla. The streets around Rolla Square and the neighbouring Al Shuwaihean blocks hold a dense run of small and medium jewellers, many Pakistani-, Indian- and Syrian-owned, most of them decades old. The stock leans to plain 22K and 21K: bangles sold in pairs, chains by the gram, bridal sets with stones kept to a minimum so the weight is what you pay for.

Why the margins are thin:

  • Rents are a fraction of Deira's, and the owners usually stand behind the counter themselves.
  • The customer base is working families from Sharjah, Ajman and northern Dubai who buy regularly and compare across the street.
  • Many shops double as wholesale suppliers to Ajman and the northern emirates, so they are used to quoting on volume.

The experience is unpolished and the bargaining is expected. Go with a weight in mind, ask for the making charge per gram, and be prepared to walk to the next door; that walk is usually what gets you the better number. The Sharjah gold souk page has a street map and timings, and making charges explains what a fair per-gram fee looks like for each type of piece.

Gold rate in Sharjah today for 22 carat: the Pakistani and Indian favourite

Sharjah has one of the largest Pakistani communities in the country alongside a big Indian one, and both traditions are 22-carat traditions. That is why Rolla's windows are heavy with 916 bangles and why shopkeepers quote in tolas as readily as grams. One tola is 11.6638 grams, so a two-tola set is a little over twenty-three grams of 22K; our calculator switches between the units so nobody has to do that sum at the counter.

A worked example without a stale number: a six-gram 22K chain is six times the 22K row on the board in metal value. In Rolla the making charge on a machine-made chain is usually a small per-gram fee; add it, then 5% VAT, and that is the invoice. If a shop quotes a single all-in price, ask them to split it; a reluctance to do so is a reason to try next door.

Buyers heading home should also check the rate in Pakistani rupees or Indian rupees pages before deciding how much to carry, since the saving in Sharjah can be eaten by customs duty on arrival. The 21K page covers the Arab families' choice, and the karat guide compares both.

A Sharjah buying checklist

The Rolla style of trading rewards buyers who arrive prepared. This is the routine experienced residents follow.

  1. Check the live board before you leave home and note the 22K or 21K gram rate. You now know the metal value of any weight.
  2. Pick two or three shops in the same street. Walk the row first without buying; note which counters have the designs you want.
  3. Have the piece weighed on the counter scale and read the display yourself. Ask whether stones or clasps are included in the weight.
  4. Ask for the making charge as a separate line. Per gram for plain pieces, percentage for ornate ones. Negotiate here, never on the metal rate.
  5. Confirm the hallmark. Look for the 916 or 875 stamp and ask about the shop's own mark. The hallmark guide shows what to expect.
  6. Get a tax invoice with weight, karat, making charge and VAT itemised, in your name. It is your proof for resale, exchange and, if you are a visitor, the airport VAT refund.
Rolla shops are at their most relaxed on weekday mornings between opening and the noon prayer. Friday afternoons and the evenings before Eid are the worst time to negotiate anything.

Living in Sharjah, working in Dubai: where to buy?

Tens of thousands of people commute daily between the two emirates, and the question of where to buy comes up at every family gathering. The metal rate settles nothing because it is the same on both sides of the Al Mamzar roundabout. So the decision comes down to three things.

Making charges. For plain 22K and 21K pieces, Rolla is usually at least as competitive as Deira and often a touch better, because of lower overheads. For intricate bridal sets, Deira's larger workshops and the Meena Bazaar specialists may undercut Sharjah on labour-heavy work simply because they make more of it.

Range. Dubai wins on choice, especially for 18K, diamonds and brand collections. If you cannot find the design in Rolla, the Deira souk is a twenty-minute drive outside rush hour.

Convenience and trust. A jeweller you have used for years in Al Nahda who will resize, clean and buy back without fuss is worth a small premium. Many commuters buy locally in Sharjah and reserve Dubai trips for big occasions. The Dubai rate page maps the districts for those trips, and gold shops near me helps you find the nearest counter on either side.

Selling gold in Sharjah at the day's rate

Sharjah is a good place to sell as well as buy, for the same reason it is a good place to buy: high-turnover shops that need stock. Rolla jewellers buy 22K and 21K at the live rate minus a margin, test the piece in front of you with acid or an XRF machine, and pay in cash or transfer. Because many of them also sell wholesale, the buy-back spread is often tighter than at a mall boutique.

Three habits protect you. Get at least two offers in the same street; the spread between shops can be wider than you expect on a quiet afternoon. Bring the original invoice if you have it, since a piece with a known hallmark and a known weight is tested faster and valued better. And remember that nobody pays for the making charge you paid years ago, so plain pieces return more of their purchase price than ornate ones.

If you would rather keep the gold and borrow against it, licensed finance companies in Sharjah and Dubai offer gold loans with the jewellery held as security. The selling guide and the gold loan page cover both routes in detail.

Questions people ask

What is the gold rate in Sharjah today for 22 carat?

The live 22 carat gram rate is on the board at the top of this page and updates while you read. It is always 91.6% of the 24K rate because 22K gold is 916 fine. Shops in Rolla, the Central Souk and the Sharjah malls all work from the same national figure; the only thing that differs between them is the making charge added on top.

Is gold cheaper in Sharjah than in Dubai?

The metal is the same price everywhere in the UAE. Sharjah's reputation for cheaper gold comes from the Rolla and Al Shuwaihean jewellers, whose low rents and regular customers keep making charges on plain 22K and 21K pieces among the lowest in the country. On heavily worked bridal sets and on 18K diamond pieces, Dubai's larger workshops can match or beat Sharjah.

Where are the gold shops in Sharjah?

The main cluster is around Rolla Square and the adjoining Al Shuwaihean streets, a few minutes' walk from the bus station. The Central (Blue) Souk on the Khalid Lagoon has a gold floor aimed at visitors. Chain brands have branches in Sahara Centre, City Centre Sharjah and Mega Mall, and neighbourhood jewellers serve Al Nahda, Al Taawun and Muwailih.

Do Sharjah gold shops quote in tolas?

Many do, because of the large Pakistani and Indian clientele, but the official rate and the invoice are per gram. One tola equals 11.6638 grams. If a shop quotes a tola price, divide by that figure to compare with the gram rate on our board, or use our calculator, which handles both units and every karat.

What are the opening hours of gold shops in Sharjah?

Most Rolla and Central Souk jewellers open mid-morning, close for a few hours in the afternoon, and reopen into the late evening, with a later start on Friday. Mall branches follow mall hours and do not take an afternoon break. Hours shift during Ramadan, when the evening session runs much later; check before a special trip.
