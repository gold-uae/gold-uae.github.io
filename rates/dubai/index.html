Gold Rate in Dubai Today: Live Price per Gram and Where to Buy

Dubai is where the UAE's gold rate is published and where most of the country's gold is actually bought, so this page does two jobs. The board below gives you the live gold rate in Dubai today for every karat, in dirhams per gram. The rest of the page tells you where in the city to take that number and how to turn it into a fair price for a real piece.

If you only remember one thing: the rate is identical in every shop in Dubai. The shops differ on making charges, range and honesty, and that is where this guide earns its keep.

Quick answer

  • The Dubai gold rate today is quoted per gram in AED and follows the international spot price minute by minute.
  • The 22 carat rate is 91.6% of the 24K figure; 21K is 87.5% and 18K is 75%.
  • Deira's Gold Souk, Meena Bazaar, Karama and the Gold & Diamond Park are the four districts worth knowing.
  • Your invoice is the gram rate times weight, plus the making charge, plus 5% VAT on jewellery.

Shops across the city display the same figures, usually on a digital screen near the entrance. Because the board tracks the dollar spot price and the dirham is pegged, an overnight move in New York shows up on Dubai boards at opening. On a volatile day, a shop that set its board at 10 a.m. may be well behind by 4 p.m., which is why we show the live number rather than a morning snapshot.

The maths behind the rows is simple: the 24K rate is the ounce price in dollars divided by 31.1035 and multiplied by the peg of AED 3.6725 per dollar; the other karats are that figure scaled by purity. The 22K gold rate page shows the 22-carat line on its own with a bigger card.

Dubai gold rate for 22 carat: why it leads the board

Ask for "the gold rate" in Deira and the answer will usually be the 22 carat gram price. Dubai's biggest buying communities are Indian, Pakistani, Sri Lankan and Bangladeshi, and their jewellery tradition is 916 fineness. Dubai's workshops and importers therefore stock 22K deepest, and 22K is also where the market is most competitive on making charges.

A worked example without a stale price: an 18-gram 22K necklace is worth eighteen times the 22K row on the board in metal. Add a making charge that for a machine-made chain might be a modest per-gram fee, or for a hand-set bridal piece might be a meaningful percentage, then 5% VAT on the total. The calculator does the first step; the shop's quote gives the second; you do the sense check.

Gulf nationals and Arab expatriates in Dubai mostly ask for 21K instead, and the city's Arab-owned shops in Satwa, Jumeirah and the Deira side streets stock it in depth. The 21K page explains the difference, and the karat guide helps you choose if you are undecided.

From the board to the bill: what Dubai shops add

The number on the board is the one thing you cannot negotiate. Everything that sits on top of it, you can.

  1. Weigh the piece in front of you. Any honest counter has a calibrated scale and will show the display. Stones and clasps are weighed as part of the piece unless the shop deducts for them; ask.
  2. Get the making charge as a separate figure. In Dubai it is quoted per gram for plain work or as a percentage for ornate pieces. Mall brands often fold it into a single price, so ask them to split it.
  3. Check for extras. Some shops add a "wastage" line on 22K bridal work. It is negotiable and often disappears if you ask calmly.
  4. Add 5% VAT. Tourists get this back at the airport under the Planet refund scheme on a qualifying purchase, so keep the tax invoice.
Visit the souk on a weekday morning, not a Friday evening. Fewer tourists, more patient staff, and the board has just been set from the overnight price, so you know exactly what you are comparing.

The making charges page lists what is typical for each kind of piece, and VAT and tax refund explains the Planet process step by step.

Is the gold price in Dubai really lower than elsewhere?

Yes for the finished piece, no for the metal. The ounce costs the same in Dubai as in London or Mumbai. What Dubai removes is the layers other countries add: there is no import duty on gold here, VAT is only 5% and refundable for visitors, and hundreds of competing shops keep making charges thin. India, by contrast, charges customs duty on imported gold and GST on jewellery, which is why the same 22K bangle can be noticeably cheaper in Deira than in Kochi.

Two caveats keep people honest. First, exchange rates move: if the rupee weakens against the dollar, Dubai gold looks cheaper in rupees even though nothing changed here. Second, what you carry home may be taxed on arrival; the duty-free allowance for returning Indian residents is small and the rest attracts customs duty. Run the numbers on the rate in Indian rupees page, and read is gold cheaper in Dubai and taking gold from Dubai to India before you plan a shopping trip around the saving.

Timing a purchase in Dubai: seasons, not predictions

We will not tell you where the gold price goes next; nobody can. What we can tell you is when Dubai's shops are most generous on the parts of the price they control.

  • Dubai Shopping Festival (winter): city-wide promotions, raffles, and reduced or waived making charges on selected lines at the big chains.
  • Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha: gift buying peaks; promotions are common, but souk counters are crowded in the final days before the holiday.
  • Diwali and Dhanteras (autumn): the 22K counters in Deira, Karama and Meena Bazaar are at their busiest; many shops launch collections and offer making-charge deals.
  • Summer (June to August): quieter shops, more room to negotiate, and plenty of stock as the wedding season pauses.

For the price itself, follow the live chart and the price analysis page, which explains what the market is reacting to without pretending to forecast it. Many long-term buyers simply buy a fixed amount at regular intervals and ignore the noise.

Selling gold in Dubai at today's rate

The same board that sets the buying price sets the selling price. Shops in Deira and Karama buy back 22K and 21K jewellery at the day's gram rate minus a small margin, paying for the metal only. If you bought the piece from that same shop and still have the invoice, most will offer a better buy-back than a stranger would, and some run exchange schemes where old gold is valued at the full rate against a new purchase.

What nobody pays for is the making charge you paid when you bought. That is why plain, heavy pieces hold value better than intricate ones, and why bars and coins bought at a small premium over 24K are the cleanest way to hold gold you may want to sell later. Questions people ask

What is the gold rate in Dubai today for 22 carat?

The live 22 carat gram rate is on the board at the top of this page and updates while you read. It is always 91.6% of the 24K rate, because 22K gold is 916 parts per thousand pure. Every shop in Dubai works from the same figure; what differs is the making charge added on top, so compare that rather than hunting for a lower gram rate.

Which area in Dubai has the cheapest gold?

The metal rate is identical everywhere in Dubai. For the lowest making charges on 22K, the Deira Gold Souk and Karama shops are the most competitive because so many counters sit side by side. Meena Bazaar is strong for Indian bridal work at fair prices. Malls and brand boutiques charge more for the same weight because you are paying for design, service and location.

Does the gold rate in Dubai include VAT?

No. The board shows the metal value per gram before tax. Jewellery attracts 5% VAT on the total invoice including the making charge. Investment bars and coins of 99% purity or higher are zero-rated. Visitors who buy jewellery can reclaim the VAT at the airport through the Planet scheme, provided the purchase meets the minimum and the goods leave the UAE within ninety days.

How many times a day does the Dubai gold rate change?

The underlying spot price moves continuously during the global trading week, so the live board here changes constantly. Physical shops typically update their displayed rate two or three times a day, more often when the market is jumping. The Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group's published reference rate is the one most shops anchor to at opening.

Can I buy gold in Dubai at the board rate without any making charge?

Only for bars and coins, which carry a small premium over 24K rather than a making charge. For jewellery there is always some workmanship cost, though promotions advertising "zero making charges" appear during the Dubai Shopping Festival and festival seasons on selected ranges. Read the conditions: the offer usually applies to particular designs and may be tied to a minimum weight.
