Half the people queuing at a Karama jeweller on a Friday evening are doing the same sum in their heads: what is this chain worth back home in Kochi, Chennai or Mumbai? This page does the sum for you. It also explains why Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra publish slightly different numbers for the same metal on the same morning.

How the gold rate in India is built, step by step

An Indian jeweller's board is the end of a longer chain than a Dubai board. Starting from the same international spot price, the metal passes through these layers before it reaches a shop in T. Nagar or Zaveri Bazaar:

  1. Dollar spot price per ounce, converted to grams.
  2. USD/INR exchange rate, which moves daily and often against the rupee.
  3. Basic customs duty on imported gold, cut to 6% in the July 2024 budget, plus the applicable cess. The effective total changes with each notification, so check the latest CBIC circular.
  4. Bullion association rates published locally, which set the day's benchmark for a city or state.
  5. 3% GST on the jewellery value, with GST also applied to making charges.

Dubai skips the import duty entirely, charges 5% VAT instead of GST, and lets tourists reclaim that VAT at the airport. That is the whole structural reason for the Dubai discount; the gold itself costs exactly the same in both countries.

Why Kerala, Chennai and Mumbai quote different numbers

Search for today's gold rate in Kerala and then for the rate in Mumbai, and the two will not match even though both describe 22K gold on the same day. Three things explain the spread.

Units

Kerala traditionally quotes per pavan, also called a sovereign, which is 8 grams of 22K. Tamil Nadu often shows the same unit. Mumbai and most northern cities quote per 10 grams of 24K or 22K. Convert everything to one gram before comparing.

Local associations and timing

Each major market has its own jewellers' association that publishes a morning rate from the previous evening's close and the rupee's opening level. A city whose association updates at noon will differ from one that updates at nine.

Transport, premiums and competition

Kerala's rate is famously tight because of fierce competition among large retail chains, while a smaller city's rate includes a wider margin. None of these differences is as large as the gap between any Indian city and the Dubai rate, but they do confuse families comparing a Kochi bill with a Chennai bill.

Is gold cheaper in Dubai than in India? The honest answer

Per gram of metal, yes, almost always, and the gap is roughly the size of India's duty and cess minus whatever VAT you cannot reclaim. But "cheaper" for a shopper depends on four things that are easy to overlook.

The rupee effect: why your home rate rises when gold does not

Here is a pattern that puzzles many expatriates. The ounce price in dollars has not moved all week, the Dubai board is unchanged, yet the gold rate in Chennai has crept higher every day. The culprit is the exchange rate. Gold in India is priced in rupees, and when the rupee weakens against the dollar, the same ounce costs more rupees.

For someone earning in dirhams this cuts both ways:

  • A weaker rupee makes your dirham salary worth more at home and makes gold bought in Dubai look even cheaper in rupee terms.
  • The same weakness raises the price of gold already sitting in a locker in India, so family holdings gain in rupee value without the metal moving.
  • A rupee rally reverses both effects at once.

This is why the conversion on this page can change even when the spot price in dollars is quiet. Watch the two separately: the gold price tells you about the metal, the exchange rate tells you about the timing of your remittance.

Bringing Dubai gold to India: allowance, duty and paperwork

The saving is only real if the gold crosses the border without surprise costs. Residents returning after at least a year abroad enjoy a duty-free allowance on gold jewellery: 20 grams up to a value cap for men and 40 grams up to a higher cap for women. The caps are in rupees and have not kept pace with the price, so at today's rates the gram limit is rarely the binding one.

Above the allowance, customs duty applies on the excess. Keep the original invoice from the Dubai shop showing weight, karat and amount paid, carry the jewellery rather than packing it in checked baggage, and declare it at the red channel if you are over the limit. Bars and coins are treated differently from jewellery and are not covered by the free allowance.

Ask the Dubai jeweller to print the purity as 916 on the invoice and to stamp each piece. Indian buyers and assayers recognise 916 immediately, which makes a later resale or exchange in Kerala or Mumbai far smoother than an unmarked piece.

The full procedure, including what counts as jewellery, is on the taking gold from Dubai to India page.

Three shopping scenarios compared in grams, not rupees

Because prices change daily, it is more useful to think in grams and percentages than in rupee figures. Consider three typical purchases made by Indian families in the UAE.

A 12-gram 22K chain for a nephew's birthday. Bought at a Bur Dubai shop during a low-making-charge offer, it falls well within the men's allowance. The saving versus Chennai is essentially the Indian duty and GST, minus the VAT if the buyer is a resident who cannot reclaim it.

A 36-gram 22K bridal set with stone work. Here making charges may be a sizeable share of the bill in both countries. Dubai still wins on the metal, but compare the making charge per gram line by line; some Kerala chains are competitive on this specific style.

Two 50-gram 24K bars as a wedding gift. Zero-rated for VAT in the UAE and cheapest per gram of all, but bars fall outside the duty-free jewellery allowance, so customs applies on arrival. Many families instead buy small coins or plain bangles that count as jewellery. The 22K rate page explains why this karat dominates the Indian market.

Questions people ask

What is the Dubai gold rate in Indian rupees today?

The live conversion is in the widget at the top of this page. It takes the Dubai per-gram rate in dirhams and converts it with the current USD/INR exchange rate, since the dirham is pegged to the dollar. The result is the rupee cost of buying in Dubai, before any Indian customs duty on return.

How much cheaper is gold in Dubai compared to Kerala?

The structural gap is India's import duty and cess plus 3% GST, minus the 5% UAE VAT if you cannot reclaim it. The exact percentage shifts with each budget notification and with the rupee. On plain 22K jewellery the Dubai advantage is clear; on intricate pieces with high making charges it narrows, so compare total bills, not just the metal rate.

Why does the gold rate in Kerala look so much higher than the Dubai rate?

Usually because Kerala quotes per pavan, which is 8 grams of 22K, while Dubai quotes per gram. Divide the Kerala figure by eight, convert the Dubai gram rate to rupees, and the two become comparable. The remaining difference is duty, GST and local margin.

Does the Indian gold rate rise when the rupee falls?

Yes. Indian gold is the dollar price multiplied by the USD/INR rate, so a weaker rupee lifts the rupee price even when gold is flat worldwide. For UAE residents this also means that a dirham salary buys more rupees on those days, which keeps Dubai gold attractive in home-currency terms.

Can I sell Dubai gold in India at the Indian rate?

Generally yes, after the jeweller tests it. Dubai 22K is stamped 916 and is widely trusted, though it does not carry the Indian BIS hallmark. Expect the buyer to pay the day's Indian metal rate for the pure gold content and nothing for the original making charges, which is the same practice as selling in the UAE.
