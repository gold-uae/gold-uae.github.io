Best Gold Shops in Dubai: Malabar, Joyalukkas, Damas and the Big Jewellers Compared

Search volumes for "Malabar gold rate" or "Joyalukkas gold rate UAE" suggest many people believe each chain publishes its own price. It does not. Every licensed jeweller in the Emirates quotes the metal from the same daily Dubai rate, so when two brands show different totals for a 15-gram necklace, every dirham of that gap is making charge, stone work or exchange policy. That is good news: it means you can judge a jeweller on the things that actually vary.

Below is a working comparison of the big names you will meet in UAE malls and souks, grouped by who they serve best, with the questions to ask at each counter.

Quick answer

  • Malabar, Joyalukkas, Damas and every other UAE jeweller use the same daily per-gram rate; the brand does not set its own.
  • Brands differ on making charges, exchange and buy-back terms, certification and design language.
  • Indian-heritage chains lead on 22K bridal sets; Gulf houses lead on 21K and 18K fashion pieces.
  • Emirates Gold is a bar and coin refiner, not a jewellery shop, so do not look for it in a mall.
  • The best gold shop in Dubai is the one whose policies suit what you will do with the piece later.

Is there really no separate Malabar gold rate or Damas gold rate?

No. The per-gram figure on a Malabar Gold & Diamonds board in Karama, a Damas board in Dubai Mall and an independent counter in Deira is the same number at the same hour, because all of them take it from the published Dubai retail rate that tracks the international spot price. What a brand can do is decide how it sells against that number. Some chains quote a per-gram making charge, some a percentage of the metal value, some a flat fee per piece. Some add a visible "value addition" line for stones, enamel or rhodium plating. Some will later buy the item back at close to the day's rate; others deduct a fat percentage. Those policies, not the rate, make one jeweller better than another for you.

Treat a search like "malabar gold rate today 22 carat uae" as a search for the 22K gold rate today plus Malabar's current making charge for the piece you want.

Malabar gold near me: where the brands actually are

The large chains cluster in three kinds of location, and knowing this saves a drive:

  • Deira and Bur Dubai streets. Flagship showrooms of the Indian chains stand on the edge of the Deira Gold Souk and along the Meena Bazaar lanes, often several floors high. Stock is deepest here.
  • Karama and Al Nahda. Residential branches sized for a family's monthly gold-scheme visit, with repairs on site.
  • Malls. Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, Deira City Centre and Sharjah's big malls host smaller, design-led branches aimed at tourists and gift buyers.

In Abu Dhabi the same names sit on Hamdan Street and around Madinat Zayed, and in Sharjah along Rolla and in the Central Souk. For a full district-by-district map, use our gold shops near me page. If a branch is tiny, ask whether the piece you want can be transferred from a larger showroom; most chains move stock between branches within a day.

Five things that make one jeweller better than another

Making charges and how they are quoted

Ask for the charge as dirhams per gram and as a percentage of the metal value, and ask whether it is negotiable. Branded stores negotiate less than souk counters but more than their price tags suggest, especially on sets. Read our making charges explainer before you go.

Exchange and buy-back policy

This is the biggest hidden difference. Some chains exchange their own hallmarked pieces for new ones at the full current metal rate with only the new making charge to pay; others deduct a percentage. Ask what happens if you sell the piece back for cash rather than exchanging it, and whether the policy is printed on the invoice.

Certification

A hallmark is a legal requirement, but chains add their own certificates for diamonds and sometimes assay cards for gold. See the hallmark guide.

Design language

Indian-heritage brands carry wider 22K stock; Gulf houses carry wider 21K and 18K. Buying 21K at a chain that mostly stocks 22K gives you a thin selection, and vice versa.

After-sales

Free cleaning, resizing and chain repair for life are standard at the big chains and worth something over the years.

Brand store or independent souk counter: when each wins

The honest answer is that both have a place, and many experienced UAE buyers use both.

Choose a brand when you want a printed exchange policy, a tourist VAT refund processed smoothly at checkout, a diamond certificate, a gift receipt, or an online order delivered to your door. Brands also win for first-time buyers who are not comfortable bargaining in a crowded lane.

Choose an independent counter when you want plain 21K or 22K at the lowest making charge, a custom piece built from a photograph, or a large bridal order where the volume gives you leverage. The manufacturers inside the Gold & Diamond Park often undercut both on custom work.

The disciplined approach is to get a brand quote first, because it is transparent, then test the souk against it. If the souk counter cannot beat the brand by a meaningful margin on an itemised invoice, the brand's policies are worth the small premium.

Photograph the brand's price tag and the itemised quote on your phone. Souk counters respond instantly to a real competing number, and brand branches will often match a rival chain's making charge on the same weight if you show it.

Malabar gold online UAE and other brand web shops

Most of the big chains run UAE online stores that price from the same daily rate, lock the price at checkout and deliver insured within the Emirates. For a resident in Ras Al Khaimah or Fujairah this is often the easiest way to buy a branded 22K chain without a long drive. A few points to check:

  1. The product page should state gross weight, karat and whether stones are included in the weight.
  2. Delivery should be insured and require ID at the door.
  3. The return window and the restocking rules for jewellery, which are tighter than for electronics.
  4. Whether online pieces carry the same exchange policy as in-store pieces.

Compare the online total with the live figure on our gold calculator to see how much making charge you are paying. The how to buy gold in Dubai guide has a full online checklist.

Seasonal offers, gold schemes and what they are worth

Branded jewellers lean on the UAE calendar. Around Diwali and Onam the Indian chains run making-charge discounts and festival collections; before Eid and through wedding season the Gulf houses push 21K sets; during the Dubai Shopping Festival and Dubai Summer Surprises there are raffles and "zero making charge on selected items" campaigns. A zero-making-charge offer is real value on plain pieces but check whether a higher value-addition line quietly replaced it.

Many chains also run monthly savings schemes where you pay instalments and redeem at the end against jewellery, sometimes with the last instalment or part of the making charge waived. These schemes tie you to one brand, so weigh the bonus against the loss of choice. If you are buying purely to hold value rather than wear, our investing section explains why bars and coins usually beat jewellery schemes.

Questions people ask

Does Malabar Gold have a different gold rate from Joyalukkas?

No. Both chains, and every other licensed jeweller in the UAE, price the metal from the same daily Dubai per-gram rate for each karat. Any difference in the final bill for an identical weight comes from the making charge, stone or value-addition charges, or a promotion, not from the gold itself. Compare their itemised quotes line by line.

Which is the best gold shop in Dubai for 22K bridal jewellery?

The Indian-heritage chains carry the deepest 22K bridal stock and their flagship showrooms near the Deira Gold Souk and Meena Bazaar hold the widest ranges. For a large order, get a brand quote, then test the independent counters in the same lanes against it. Many families end up buying the main set from a brand and the smaller pieces from the souk.

Is Emirates Gold a jewellery shop?

No. Emirates Gold is a refinery operating in the Dubai free zone that produces bars and coins carrying its stamp. You buy its products through bullion dealers, some jewellers and exchange houses rather than at a branded showroom. If you want investment gold rather than jewellery, start with our gold bars and gold coins pages.

Can I exchange old gold at a branded jeweller?

Yes, all the large chains accept old gold against new purchases. If the old piece carries their own hallmark and invoice, most will credit the full current metal rate; for other gold they test it and typically deduct a small percentage. Ask for the policy in writing before you hand over the piece, and compare it with the cash offer on our selling pages.

Are branded jewellers more expensive than the Gold Souk?

On plain 21K and 22K pieces, usually yes by a modest margin, because branded stores carry higher fixed making charges and negotiate less. On diamond and designer pieces the gap narrows or reverses, and brands add exchange guarantees and after-sales that independent counters rarely match. The right choice depends on what you plan to do with the piece later.
