VAT on Gold in the UAE: Rates, Tourist Refunds and the Reverse Charge Explained

Tax on gold in Dubai is light, but it is not zero, and the details decide how much you really pay. Since 1 January 2018 the UAE has applied a 5% value added tax, and gold sits in two different boxes: jewellery is taxed at the standard rate, while investment gold of 99% purity or more that is traded on its metal value is zero-rated. Tourists can get the 5% on jewellery back at the airport, residents cannot, and businesses trading gold with each other use a reverse-charge mechanism that keeps cash from being tied up in tax.

This page walks through each case with the practical steps, so you know what the invoice should show and what you can claim.

Quick answer

  • The UAE charges 5% VAT on gold jewellery and zero on investment-grade bars and coins.
  • There is no import duty, income tax or capital gains tax on gold for individuals.
  • Tourists reclaim the 5% through Planet at departure: minimum AED 250 per invoice, 90 days to leave.
  • Registered traders settle VAT on gold between themselves through the reverse charge.
  • Keep the tax invoice: it is needed for the refund, for resale and for customs at home.

Is gold tax free in Dubai? Separating the four taxes

When people ask whether gold is tax free in Dubai they usually mean four different taxes at once, and the answer differs for each.

  • Import duty: none on gold entering the UAE. This is the biggest reason the Dubai retail rate undercuts India, Pakistan and Egypt.
  • VAT: 5% on jewellery, and on the making charge and any stone charge that comes with it. Zero on investment-grade bars and coins.
  • Income and capital gains tax: none for individuals. If you buy a bar and sell it later at a higher rate, the gain is yours in full.
  • Customs at home: not a UAE tax at all, but it is the one that bites travellers. India, for example, allows a modest duty-free jewellery allowance for returning residents and charges duty above it.

So the honest summary is: gold is duty-free and gains are untaxed, jewellery carries a refundable 5%, and bullion carries nothing. The four-karat rate strip below is the metal value before any of that is added:

VAT on gold jewellery in the UAE versus investment gold

The line between the two categories is drawn by purity and purpose, not by shape alone.

ItemVAT treatmentWhy
22K, 21K or 18K jewellery5% on the full invoiceBelow 99% purity; sold as a finished consumer good
24K necklace or bangle worn as jewelleryUsually 5%Purity qualifies but it is traded as jewellery, with making charges
Bars and coins of 99% purity or higherZero-ratedInvestment-grade precious metal traded on its metal value
Making charge on taxed jewellery5%Part of the price of a taxed supply
Stones set in jewellery5%Taxed with the piece

Zero-rated is not the same as exempt. A zero-rated supply is still inside the VAT system, which lets the dealer recover VAT on its own costs and is why bullion shops can quote such tight premiums over spot. It also means your bar invoice should show VAT at 0% rather than no VAT line at all.

For a resident deciding between a 50 g bar and 50 g of bangles, the tax difference alone is 5% of the bangle price, before the making charge. That is a large part of why the bars guide recommends bullion for pure savings and the 22K or 21K pages for jewellery you intend to wear.

Tourist VAT refund on gold in Dubai: step by step

Visitors can recover the VAT paid on jewellery through the national tax-free scheme operated by Planet. The procedure is the same at every UAE airport and the main land and sea exits.

  1. Buy from a registered shop. Look for the tax-free signage or ask. Most jewellers in the Deira souk, the malls and the Gold & Diamond Park are enrolled.
  2. Show your passport at the counter. The shop issues a digital tax-free tag linked to the invoice. The invoice must be for at least AED 250 and in your name.
  3. Keep the gold with you, unworn and with the invoice. You may be asked to present the goods when validating.
  4. Validate before check-in. At the airport, go to the Planet validation point or self-service kiosk before you drop your bag. Scan your passport and confirm the tags. Export must happen within 90 days of purchase.
  5. Choose how to receive the refund. Card refund takes a few days; cash is available at some exits in dirhams, with a cap.

A service fee is deducted from the VAT amount, so you receive most but not all of the 5%. A tourist from Lahore buying a 6 g pair of 22K earrings will get back a small but real sum; on a 40 g wedding set it becomes meaningful.

Do the validation first, before anything else at the airport. Once a bag containing the gold has been checked in, the refund for that tag can be refused because the goods cannot be inspected.

Where the gold VAT refund gets lost

The scheme is efficient, yet a share of tourists leave without their money. The common reasons are all avoidable.

  • The shop was not registered. Small souk shops are sometimes outside the scheme. Ask before paying, not after.
  • The invoice is in the wrong name or has no tag. The tag must be created at the time of sale; it cannot be added a week later.
  • Below the minimum. Two separate invoices under AED 250 each do not combine.
  • Residents and GCC nationals living in the UAE. The refund is for visitors; UAE residents are not eligible even if they fly out.
  • Leaving after 90 days. The tag expires.
  • Wearing the piece through the airport without the invoice. Validation may require seeing the goods alongside the paperwork.
  • Checking the bag first. The single most common mistake, as above.

If a refund is refused at the exit, it is rarely recoverable later, so plan twenty extra minutes at the airport. Residents who cannot claim should not feel short-changed: the making charge is where most of their saving lives, and no refund scheme in the world beats a well-negotiated per-gram fee.

VAT reverse charge on gold in the UAE: how traders handle it

Between VAT-registered businesses, gold is not sold with 5% added to the invoice and paid over to the seller. Instead the buyer accounts for the VAT on its own return under a reverse-charge mechanism, declaring the tax as both output and input in the same period. The cash effect is neutral, which matters enormously in a trade where a single transaction can be worth millions of dirhams and margins are measured in fractions of a percent.

The mechanism applies when both parties are registered, the buyer intends to resell or process the metal, and the invoice states that the reverse charge applies. The supplier must verify the buyer's registration and keep the written declaration on file. If any condition fails, the normal 5% charge applies and the supplier is liable.

In recent years the Federal Tax Authority has broadened the scope of this treatment beyond gold to other precious metals and precious stones, including diamonds, and to jewellery made of them when traded between registered businesses, with the paperwork requirements tightened at the same time. The wholesalers and factories page describes the commercial side of that market; traders should rely on the FTA's current decision and guide rather than any summary, since the conditions and the list of covered goods are updated by cabinet decision.

VAT when you sell, exchange or inherit gold

The tax questions do not end at the purchase.

Selling your own gold

A private individual selling jewellery or a bar to a shop is not making a taxable supply, so no VAT is charged on what you receive. The shop pays you the metal value, usually at or just under the board rate. The selling guide covers what to expect at the counter.

Exchanging old gold for new

This is the most common transaction in the souk and the one where VAT confuses people. The new piece is a taxable supply in full; your old gold is a payment, not a discount on the taxable amount. Ask the shop to show the VAT line clearly on the exchange invoice and to credit your old gold at a stated per-gram rate.

Gifts and inheritance

No tax arises in the UAE when gold passes within a family. Keep the original invoices with the pieces, because they establish karat and weight for the day someone sells or travels with them.

Gold loans

Pledging gold against a loan is not a sale; the piece stays yours and no VAT is involved on the metal, though lender fees may carry VAT. See gold loans in the UAE.

Reading the tax invoice: what a correct one shows

Every VAT-registered shop must issue a tax invoice, and for gold it should contain the following, whether you bought a ring in Meena Bazaar or a kilobar from a Deira dealer.

  • The words "Tax Invoice", the shop's name, address and tax registration number.
  • Date and a unique invoice number.
  • Description of each item: karat and purity (916, 875, 750 or 999), net gold weight in grams, stone details if any.
  • Rate per gram applied and the resulting metal value.
  • Making charge as its own line, where applicable.
  • VAT rate and VAT amount per line: 5% for jewellery, 0% for qualifying bullion.
  • Total payable, and the tax-free tag reference for tourists.

Compare the rate per gram with the live rate on the Dubai rate page at the moment of purchase. If a shop issues only a handwritten receipt with a single total, you have no VAT line to reclaim and no proof of karat for resale. Insist on the proper document; registered shops produce it in seconds. For bullion, add the refiner, bar serial number and assay certificate reference to that list, because those are what a future buyer will want to see.

Questions people ask

How much is VAT on gold in the UAE?

The standard rate is 5%, applied to gold jewellery including its making charge and any stones. Investment-grade gold, meaning bars and coins of at least 99% purity traded on their metal value, is zero-rated, so the invoice shows 0% VAT. There is no separate import duty, sales tax or capital gains tax on gold for individuals in the UAE.

Can tourists get a VAT refund on gold bought in Dubai?

Yes. Buy from a shop registered with the Planet tax-free scheme, show your passport so a tag is issued against an invoice of at least AED 250, then validate the tag at the airport before check-in within 90 days of purchase. A service fee is deducted and the balance is refunded to your card or, at some exits, in cash. Residents are not eligible.

Is there VAT on gold bars and coins in Dubai?

Bars and coins of 99% purity or higher that are traded on their gold content are zero-rated, so you pay no VAT. The invoice still lists VAT at 0%, which is a sign the dealer is treating the sale correctly. A 24K item sold as jewellery with a making charge is normally taxed at 5% even though the purity qualifies, because it is being sold as a finished ornament.

What is the VAT reverse charge on gold?

It is a mechanism for trade between VAT-registered businesses. Instead of the seller charging 5% and the buyer paying it, the buyer declares the VAT on its own return as both output and input tax, so no cash changes hands for the tax. It requires both parties to be registered, the buyer to intend resale or processing, and the invoice to state that the reverse charge applies.

Do I pay VAT when I exchange old gold for new?

The new piece is taxed in full at 5%; your old gold is treated as part payment, not as a reduction of the taxable value. The shop should show the VAT on the full price of the new item and credit your old gold at a stated per-gram rate on the same invoice. Ask for both figures in writing so the exchange is transparent and the invoice is valid for the future.
