Gold Wholesalers in Dubai: Wholesale Gold Rate, Factories and Factory Outlets Explained

Walk through the Deira Gold Souk and look up: the floors above the glittering counters are where the wholesale trade happens. Offices with a buzzer, a scale and a safe move kilobars and trays of unfinished bangles between refiners, manufacturers and the retailers downstairs. Searches for "wholesale gold rate in Dubai" imagine a cheaper price list hidden up there. The reality is simpler and more useful: the metal costs what the world market says, and the wholesale advantage is thin margins on labour and volume.

This page explains how that trade is organised, who is allowed to buy from it, and how an ordinary resident can get factory pricing without a trade licence.

Quick answer

  • There is no secret wholesale gold rate: wholesalers trade at the international spot price plus or minus a small per-gram premium.
  • Dubai's wholesale trade sits upstairs in Deira, inside the Gold Souk Extension, in Al Quoz workshops and the DMCC free zone.
  • Most wholesalers sell only to licensed traders; individuals get close through factory outlets and manufacturer showrooms.
  • Business-to-business gold uses the VAT reverse charge, so the invoice looks different from a retail bill.
  • The real saving at a factory is on making charges, not on the metal.

What the wholesale gold rate in Dubai actually is

Retail shops quote a per-gram rate for each karat from the daily Dubai board. Wholesalers and refiners work one step closer to the source: they quote the international spot price per troy ounce, convert it to dirhams at the fixed peg, and add or subtract a premium measured in a few dirhams per gram depending on form, quantity and how quickly the deal settles. Three things follow from that. First, there is no separate wholesale number to hunt for; the spot price page shows you what the trade sees. Second, the wholesale premium on a kilobar is tiny compared with the making charge on a bangle, so the "saving" everyone imagines lives in labour, not metal. Third, wholesale prices move minute by minute, while the retail board moves a few times a day, so a wholesale quote has a short life and is usually confirmed by phone or message before settlement.

Where Dubai's gold wholesalers and factories are

Deira: upstairs and in the Gold Souk Extension

The old souk at Al Ras and the covered Gold Souk Extension host trading offices on the upper floors and in the newer annex. These are the bullion and plain-jewellery wholesalers who supply the counters below and much of the rest of the UAE. Our Dubai Gold Souk guide describes the ground floor; the offices above are reached by lifts in the side entrances.

Gold & Diamond Park and Al Quoz

Manufacturing with casting, polishing and stone-setting lives on the Al Quoz side of Sheikh Zayed Road, around the Gold & Diamond Park and the industrial blocks behind it. Many factories keep a small showroom at the front, which is where the "factory outlet" idea comes from.

DMCC and Jumeirah Lakes Towers

The free zone that regulates the bullion trade sits in JLT. Refiners, bar brands and the commodity exchange are headquartered there, and their customers are banks, exchange houses and large dealers rather than walk-in shoppers. The trading online page covers that side.

Sharjah and Ajman

Smaller workshops behind Rolla and near the Ajman souk make the chains and plain bangles that fill the northern emirates' counters, often at lower labour rates than Dubai.

Who can buy from a gold wholesaler

Most wholesale desks sell to businesses holding a UAE trade licence with a gold or jewellery activity, a tax registration number, and an established account. The reasons are practical rather than snobbish:

  • VAT reverse charge. Business-to-business gold trade in the UAE uses a reverse-charge mechanism: the buyer accounts for the VAT instead of the seller adding it. That only works between two registered businesses, so an individual cannot be invoiced that way. Our VAT guide explains the retail side.
  • Anti-money-laundering rules. Dealers in precious metals must identify their counterparties, record large transactions and report suspicious ones. Account opening is their compliance file.
  • Minimum quantities. Wholesale is priced for kilobars and trays, not one chain.

That said, an individual is not locked out of the metal itself. Investment bars and coins are sold to anyone at close to spot by bullion dealers and some souk counters, zero-rated for VAT when they are 99% pure or better. See gold bars in Dubai. The wholesale door that stays shut to individuals is the one for unfinished and bulk jewellery.

Gold factory outlets in Dubai: what the label really means

Names like "Al Barakah gold factory" or "Capri gold Dubai jewel factory outlet" show up constantly in searches because residents hope a factory will sell them the same bangle the mall sells, minus the mall. Sometimes that is exactly what happens, so it is worth understanding the model rather than any single name.

A factory outlet is a manufacturer's own showroom, usually attached to a workshop in Al Quoz, the Gold & Diamond Park or an upper floor in Deira. It sells finished pieces from its own production, often with a narrower design range than a retailer and sometimes with lots of near-identical items. Because there is no retailer margin in the chain, the making charge is usually the lowest you will find for that type of work, while the metal is priced from the same daily rate as everywhere else.

Treat any outlet like any other shop: check the trade licence, the hallmark on each piece, the scale and the itemised invoice, as described in our buying guide. A factory selling retail must charge you 5% VAT on jewellery and give a tax invoice, exactly as a mall store does. If an outlet offers to skip the VAT for cash, you are not getting a wholesale deal, you are being offered an unrecorded sale, and you lose your proof of purchase.

Starting a gold business: how traders buy at wholesale

Residents who want to retail gold, run a small jewellery line or export to home markets often ask how to get a wholesale account. The outline, which you should confirm with the licensing authority and a tax adviser, is:

  1. Licence. Obtain a trade licence with a precious-metals or jewellery activity from a Dubai mainland authority or a free zone such as DMCC. The activity must match what you buy and sell.
  2. Tax registration. Register for VAT and keep records that support reverse-charge invoices.
  3. Compliance file. Expect your wholesaler to collect your licence, passport copies of owners and a source-of-funds declaration, and to refuse cash above the reporting threshold.
  4. Account and credit. New accounts settle in advance; credit terms come with history.
  5. Sourcing. Plain gold from Deira desks, finished jewellery from Al Quoz and Sharjah workshops, bars from DMCC-based refiners.

Exporting to India, Pakistan or Africa adds destination-country duties and reporting; our page on taking gold from Dubai to India explains why personal baggage is not a trade route.

Risks and red flags in the wholesale world

The upper floors of Deira are overwhelmingly honest businesses supervised by a regulator, but the wholesale label attracts a fringe. Watch for these:

  • "Wholesale rate" offers to strangers. A desk that sells to anyone with cash at below the published rate is either moving unhallmarked metal or quoting a karat it does not deliver.
  • No hallmark on finished pieces. Wholesale jewellery should still carry the purity stamp. Our hallmark guide shows what to look for.
  • Cash-only, no invoice. This removes your protection and may put you on the wrong side of reporting rules.
  • Courier "wholesale" from abroad. Gold sent to you from overseas at a bargain price is the oldest scam in the trade.
If you want factory prices as an individual, bring a drawing or a photo of the piece you want to a manufacturer's showroom in the Gold & Diamond Park, ask for a quote split into metal at today's rate and labour per gram, and compare it with two souk counters. The factory usually wins on custom work and loses on a single stock chain.

Questions people ask

What is the wholesale gold rate in Dubai today?

It is the international spot price per ounce converted to dirhams at the fixed peg, plus or minus a small per-gram premium that depends on form and quantity. Wholesalers do not publish a separate board; the live spot figure on our rates pages is the number they watch. The gap between wholesale and retail on plain gold is small; the gap on labour is where the savings sit.

Can an individual buy gold at wholesale price in Dubai?

Not through the business-to-business channel, which requires a trade licence, VAT registration and a compliance account because of the reverse-charge mechanism and anti-money-laundering rules. Individuals can buy investment bars at close to spot from bullion dealers and can get low making charges from manufacturer showrooms and factory outlets. Those two routes capture most of the wholesale advantage.

Is a gold factory outlet cheaper than the Gold Souk?

On the metal, no: both use the same daily rate. On making charges, a factory outlet is usually cheaper for its own designs and clearly cheaper for custom work, while a souk counter may be cheaper for a single machine-made chain it buys in bulk. Compare itemised quotes rather than headline promises, and insist on hallmark, invoice and VAT at either.

Where are the gold factories in Dubai?

Most manufacturing sits on the Al Quoz side of Sheikh Zayed Road, in and around the Gold & Diamond Park and the industrial blocks behind it, with some workshops on upper floors in Deira. Refiners and bar producers are based in the DMCC free zone in Jumeirah Lakes Towers. Smaller workshops in Sharjah and Ajman supply the northern emirates.

Do wholesalers charge VAT on gold?

Between two VAT-registered businesses the UAE applies a reverse-charge mechanism, so the seller does not add VAT and the buyer accounts for it in its own return. Investment-grade gold of 99% purity or better is zero-rated for everyone. Jewellery sold to an individual, even by a factory, carries the standard 5% VAT and must come with a tax invoice.
