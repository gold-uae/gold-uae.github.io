Is Gold Cheaper in Dubai? The Honest Comparison with India, the UK and Beyond

Yes, gold is usually cheaper in Dubai than in India, Pakistan, the UK and most of Europe, but not for the reason many visitors think. The gold itself is not discounted: a gram of 24K costs the same in Deira as it does in Mumbai or Birmingham once you convert the currency, because every market prices off the same international ounce. What makes the Dubai gold price lower at the till is everything wrapped around the metal: no import duty, a 5% VAT that tourists can reclaim, and the thinnest making charges of any major market.

This page puts numbers on that logic without quoting a rate that will be stale tomorrow, and tells you exactly when the trip pays for itself and when it does not.

Quick answer

  • The metal costs the same worldwide; Dubai is cheaper because of low tax and no import duty.
  • Against India, the saving comes from avoiding customs duty and GST and from lower making charges.
  • Against the UK, the main gap is VAT on jewellery and Dubai's refund for tourists.
  • The saving shrinks fast if you exceed your home customs allowance.
  • Bars and coins are the cheapest form; jewellery saves less because of making charges.

Why is gold cheaper in Dubai? Three layers, one of them fixed

Think of any gold price as a stack. At the bottom is the metal value: grams multiplied by purity multiplied by the world spot price. That layer is identical everywhere on earth, give or take a currency conversion and a few seconds of market movement. Nobody in Dubai gets the metal cheaper than a jeweller in Delhi or London does.

The second layer is government: import duty, sales tax, VAT, GST, cess and whatever else a treasury attaches to gold crossing its border or counter. The UAE levies no import duty on gold and only 5% VAT on jewellery, which a departing tourist can reclaim. India stacks customs duty and GST on top of the metal; the UK applies a standard VAT rate several times higher than the UAE's to jewellery.

The third layer is the trade: making charges, retailer margin and brand premium. Dubai's density of shops, from the Deira souk to Sharjah's Rolla to the Madinat Zayed centre in Abu Dhabi, keeps this layer thin because every shop can see its neighbour's price. So the real question is never "is the gold cheaper" but "how much do the second and third layers cost where I live". The spot price page shows the fixed layer; the rest of this guide deals with the two that vary.

Duty and tax. Gold imported commercially into India pays basic customs duty, cut to 6% in the July 2024 budget, plus cess and then GST at the shop. That combination is the single largest reason an Indian retail rate sits above the Dubai rate in rupee terms. Check the current CBIC notice, because the effective rate changes.

Making charges. A plain 22K chain in a Dubai souk typically carries a lower per-gram charge than the same chain in an Indian city, and bridal sets show the widest gap because labour and rent here are spread across enormous volumes.

The catch. A returning resident who has lived abroad a year may bring 20 g of jewellery duty-free (men) or 40 g (women), with value caps. Above that, duty applies at the airport and the Dubai advantage shrinks or disappears. For a tourist on a short visit, the allowance is lower still. The customs guide works through the limits, and the rate in rupees page tracks the daily gap.

Is Dubai gold cheaper than the UK and Europe?

For a visitor from London, Manchester or Paris, the comparison looks different because the metal rate in pounds or euros already includes no import duty on investment gold in those countries either. The gap opens up on jewellery, where European VAT rates are high, and on making charges, where European workshops charge far more per gram than a Deira bench.

So is Dubai gold cheaper than the UK? For high-purity jewellery, clearly yes, especially after the Planet VAT refund. For a 1 oz investment coin, the difference is small because both markets exempt or zero-rate it; what you gain in Dubai is a tighter dealer premium and a wider range of refiners, which the coins guide covers. Remember that your own customs authority may want to know about gold you bring home above a certain value; declare it and keep the invoice.

Is it worth buying gold in Dubai? Run the three-question test

Before you plan a purchase around a trip, ask yourself three things.

  1. What form? Bars and coins give the biggest percentage saving because there is no making charge to pay and no VAT. Plain 21K or 22K jewellery comes next. Heavy designer or diamond-set pieces save the least, because the making charge and stone pricing dominate the bill wherever you buy.
  2. What quantity? The saving on 8 g is real but modest in absolute dirhams; on a 40 g bridal set or a 100 g bar it becomes significant. Use the gold calculator to turn grams into a dirham figure, then compare it with a quote from home.
  3. What allowance? If what you plan to carry exceeds your home country's duty-free limit, add that duty to the Dubai price before comparing. For many Indian visitors on short trips, this single step decides the answer.

If the form is high-purity, the quantity is meaningful and you stay within the allowance, the trip is worth it even after a modest cost for travel. If you are buying one 18K pendant on holiday, enjoy the choice and the experience but do not expect a windfall.

Get a written quote from a jeweller at home for the exact piece type, weight and karat before you fly. Comparing a real home price with a real Dubai receipt beats any article, including this one.

Cheapest place to buy gold in Dubai: souk, mall or factory?

Within the city the metal rate does not change, so "cheapest" means lowest making charge and lowest premium.

Deira Gold Souk and Meena Bazaar

The traditional rows win for plain 21K and 22K jewellery. Dozens of shops within a few hundred metres compete on the per-gram charge, and bargaining is expected. The Deira souk guide explains how to navigate it.

Gold & Diamond Park

Manufacturers sell on site, so for custom designs or larger orders the charge can be lower than retail. It is calmer, air-conditioned and better for diamond-set work.

Mall jewellers and brands

Rarely the cheapest, but during Eid, Diwali and the Dubai Shopping Festival their zero-making-charge promotions can beat the souk for specific weights. Read the conditions.

Bullion dealers

For bars and coins, licensed dealers in Deira and the DMCC area quote the tightest spread over spot, especially on bank transfer. The bars page lists what to check on the seal and serial number.

In short, the cheapest place depends on the form; for jewellery it is almost always a well-compared souk shop.

Where the Dubai discount disappears

A few situations wipe out the advantage, and honest planning means knowing them.

  • Buying above your customs allowance. Duty at home can exceed the whole saving. Weigh what you intend to carry before you shop.
  • Paying a high making charge without comparing. A tourist who buys the first bangle at the first shop can pay a per-gram charge that erases the tax advantage.
  • Accepting a stone-heavy piece priced by gross weight. If the gold rate is applied to stones, you overpay regardless of city.
  • Forgetting the VAT refund steps. Tourists who skip the Planet validation at the airport leave 5% behind. Follow the refund procedure.
  • Buying a brand for the brand. A branded 18K piece with a premium making charge is priced on design, not metal, and resale returns metal value only.

None of these is a reason to avoid Dubai; they are reasons to buy here the way residents do: compare, weigh, negotiate the making charge, keep the invoice, and know the limit at the other end.

Gold price comparison Dubai vs the rest: what stays constant

Because the dirham is pegged at 3.6725 to the dollar, the Dubai gold rate is effectively the dollar spot price divided into grams. That makes Dubai a clean benchmark: when the rupee, the pound or the Pakistani rupee weakens against the dollar, gold at home gets dearer in local currency even if the ounce price is flat, while the Dubai price in AED does not move at all. Many South Asian families in the UAE time remittances and gold purchases around this currency effect rather than around the ounce itself.

Two more constants help. First, the UAE rate is the same in all seven emirates, so a buyer in Ajman or Al Ain is not missing a better Dubai price; only the making charge differs by neighbourhood. Second, the rate is published in the open and revised through the day, which means a visitor can verify any shop's board against the live Dubai rate and the rates in PKR, NPR and LKR in a few seconds. Transparency is itself a discount: it is hard to overcharge someone who can see the real number on a phone.

What does not stay constant is the making charge, and that is the lever you control. Treat the metal as a given, and spend your attention on everything above it.

Questions people ask

Is gold really cheaper in Dubai than in India?

At the counter, yes, in most periods: India adds customs duty and GST to the metal, and Indian retail making charges tend to be higher than in Dubai's souks. The saving is largest on plain 22K jewellery and on bars. It is smaller or negative once you exceed the duty-free allowance on return to India, because the duty collected at the airport can be larger than the price difference.

Is gold cheap in Dubai because it is lower purity?

No. Dubai's hallmarking is strict and the standard karats are 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K, each stamped with its purity number. The lower price has nothing to do with purity; it comes from zero import duty, a low refundable VAT and competitive making charges. If anything, the souks sell a higher share of 21K and 22K pieces than most Western markets, where 9K and 14K are common.

How much can I save buying gold in Dubai compared to the UK?

On jewellery the gap is roughly the difference between UK VAT and a refundable 5%, plus a usually lower making charge, which together can be a meaningful share of the price on a high-purity piece. On investment coins and bars the saving is small, since both markets exempt or zero-rate them; you gain mainly on dealer premium and choice. Factor in travel and any declaration requirements at home.

Is it cheaper to buy gold in Dubai or in Sharjah and Abu Dhabi?

The per-gram rate is identical in every emirate, so there is no metal-price advantage in any city. Sharjah's Rolla and Central Souk and Abu Dhabi's Madinat Zayed centre often match or undercut Dubai on making charges for plain pieces because rents are lower. Dubai wins on sheer choice and on the number of shops you can compare in one walk.

Does the time of year change how cheap gold is in Dubai?

The metal rate follows world markets, not the local calendar. What changes seasonally is the making charge: promotions before Eid, around Diwali and during the Dubai Shopping Festival cut or waive it on many pieces, while quiet summer weeks make souk shops more willing to negotiate. If you can choose your travel dates, those windows improve the saving without any bet on the price.
