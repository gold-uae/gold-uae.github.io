It is quoted per gram in dirhams, moves with the international spot price all day, and is the same on every shop board from Dubai to Ras Al Khaimah.

How the 22K price is calculated from spot

There is nothing mysterious about the 22K figure. It is derived from the international price of pure gold in three short steps, and you can repeat them on your phone to check any board.

  1. Take the spot price of gold in US dollars per troy ounce (see the USD spot page).
  2. Divide by 31.1035 to get a per-gram price, then multiply by 3.6725 to convert to dirhams. That gives the 24K per-gram rate.
  3. Multiply by 0.916, because 22 carat gold is 22 parts gold in 24, which is 91.6% fine.

The Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group publishes a retail rate built the same way, with a small premium for the local market, and that is what the shop boards show. Because the dirham has been pegged to the dollar since 1997, there is no currency wobble in this chain: when the ounce price in New York or London moves 1%, the 22K gram rate in Karama moves 1% too.

The practical result is that the gap between 24K and 22K is fixed by purity, not by fashion. If 24K is the ceiling, 22K sits at 91.6% of it, 21K at 87.5% and 18K at 75%, every day of the year.

What 22K gold actually is: 916 purity explained

A piece stamped 916 or 22K contains 916 parts gold per thousand. The other 84 parts are an alloy, most often copper with a little silver, which is what gives 22 carat jewellery its slightly deeper, warmer yellow than 24K and, more importantly, enough hardness to hold a chain link or a bangle's shape through years of daily wear.

Why jewellers love it

Pure 24K is soft enough to scratch with a fingernail and bends under its own weight in a thin chain. 22K keeps nearly all of the gold value while being workable enough for filigree, temple designs and heavy Kerala-style sets. That balance is why Indian and Sri Lankan jewellery traditions settled on 22K generations ago and why UAE shops serving those communities stock it by the kilo.

22K versus 24K and 21K: which should you buy?

The question behind most 22 carat gold rate searches is really a buying decision. Here is how it usually splits in the UAE.

  • Buying for a wedding or to take to India, Pakistan or Sri Lanka: 22K, without much debate. It is what families expect, what resale shops back home price easily, and what the hallmark system in India is built around.
  • Buying a bar or coin to hold value: 24K. You pay for pure metal and nothing else, and investment-grade bars are zero-rated for VAT. See the 24K gold rate page.
  • Buying Gulf-style pieces for daily wear or gifting within the Arab community: 21K is the local favourite; it is a touch harder than 22K and priced a few percent lower per gram. The 21K page explains the trade-off.
  • Diamond-set rings and designer chains: 18K, because stones need firm prongs.

A useful way to think about it: the karat decides how much metal you are paying for per gram, the design decides how much work you are paying for on top. A plain 22K bangle is one of the most efficient ways to convert salary into gold in Dubai because making charges on simple designs are low and the resale market is deep.

What the 22 carat rate means in real purchases

The per-gram rate is abstract until you multiply it by the weights people really buy. Use the gold calculator for exact figures; here is how the arithmetic looks in words.

  • A 4 gram 22K ring: four times the rate, plus a making charge that on a plain band is often a modest fixed sum per gram, plus VAT. The metal is the bulk of the bill.
  • A 16 gram 22K chain: sixteen times the rate. Machine-made chains carry low making charges; handmade rope or Singapore styles cost more per gram to produce.
  • A 48 gram 22K bridal set: the metal alone is a serious sum, and this is where making charges, sometimes quoted as a percentage, start to matter more than a half-dirham difference in the rate.

Two habits save money. First, note the board rate when you walk in, because it can change while you are choosing. Second, ask for the weight, the rate and the making charge as three separate lines on the receipt. Read the making charges guide before you negotiate, since that line is the only one with real room to move.

Where to buy 22K gold in the UAE

22 carat is everywhere, but some places specialise in it.

  • Deira Gold Souk and the Gold Souk Extension: the largest choice of 22K bridal sets, chains and bangles in the country, with heavy South Asian designs on every second counter. Take the Metro Green Line to Gold Souq station. The Gold Souk guide covers timings and how to haggle.
  • Meena Bazaar and Karama, Bur Dubai: smaller shops, strong on everyday 22K pieces and quick repairs, popular with residents who do not want the souk crowds.
  • Madinat Zayed, Abu Dhabi: the capital's gold centre, with the same rate and a big selection of 22K.
  • Sharjah Rolla and the Central Souk, Ajman souk, Al Ain souk: good for residents of the northern emirates and often quieter for negotiating making charges.
  • Large jewellery chains in malls: transparent pricing, fixed making charges, easy exchanges, slightly less room to bargain.

The rate will not differ between these places; service, design range and making charges will. The how to buy gold in Dubai guide walks through a first visit step by step.

Reading a shop board and spotting a bad quote

Every gold shop in the UAE shows a board with four or five lines: 24K, 22K, 21K, 18K and sometimes 14K, each a per-gram price in AED. The board is the metal rate the shop will use that moment, and it should match the published Dubai rate closely.

Photograph the shop board with your phone before you start trying things on. If the quote at the counter uses a higher rate than the board, you have the evidence in your hand and the conversation ends quickly.

Things that quietly raise a 22K price:

  • A making charge quoted as a percentage of the total rather than per gram, which inflates with the rate.
  • Stones, beads or enamel weighed as if they were gold. Ask for the net gold weight.
  • VAT quoted as included when it has in fact been added twice, or a "discount" that is just the VAT being removed and re-added.
  • A buy-back promise with no written terms. A fair shop will state the buy-back deduction clearly.

Tourists can reclaim the 5% VAT on 22K jewellery at the airport through the refund scheme if the piece leaves the country within 90 days; the VAT and refund page explains the paperwork.

Selling 22K gold: what the rate means on the way out

When you sell 22K jewellery in the UAE, the buyer starts from the same board rate but works downward. They test the piece, weigh it net of stones, apply the 22K fineness and then deduct a small margin for melting and resale. You never recover making charges or VAT, which is why plain, heavy 22K pieces hold value better than ornate ones.

The resale market for 22K is the deepest of any karat in the UAE, simply because so much of it exists: a souk buyer can turn a 22K chain into stock within hours. That liquidity is a quiet advantage over 18K, which often has to be melted. See the guide to selling gold in Dubai for who pays best and what to bring.

If you are taking 22K pieces to India, the duty-free allowance for returning residents is limited by weight and value, so a kilo of bangles is not a plan. The customs page has the current limits and the receipts you need to keep.

Questions people ask

Is the 22K gold rate the same in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?

Yes. The published UAE retail rate is one figure per karat used across all seven emirates, so a 22K gram costs the same metal price in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah or Fujairah on any given moment. Shops may round slightly differently and their making charges vary a lot, but the 22 carat metal rate is national, not local.

Why is 22K cheaper per gram than 24K?

Because a gram of 22K contains only 0.916 grams of gold; the rest is copper and silver alloy. Shops price it at 91.6% of the 24K rate, so the discount is purity, not a bargain. Per gram of actual gold content the two karats cost the same, and that is also how a buyer will value the piece when you sell it.

Does the 22 carat rate change during the day?

It does. The UAE rate tracks the international spot price, which trades almost around the clock on weekdays. Shop boards are updated once or several times a day, and the live card on this page updates more often than that. On big news days the morning and evening rates can differ noticeably, so lock in a quote before you leave the counter.

Is 22K gold a good investment in the UAE?

It can hold value well, especially plain pieces with low making charges, but it is not the most efficient form. Jewellery carries making charges and 5% VAT that you do not get back, whereas 24K bars are zero-rated and sold close to the metal price. Many residents buy 22K to wear and bars to hold, and treat the jewellery's value as a bonus rather than the main plan.

How do I check that a piece is really 22K?

Look for a 916 or 22K stamp, ask the shop to test it on an XRF machine in front of you, and insist on a receipt that records the karat, net weight and rate. Established UAE jewellers test routinely and will not object. Our hallmark and purity test guide explains the stamps and the simple checks you can do at home.
