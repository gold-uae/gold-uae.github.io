21K Gold Rate Today in UAE: 21 Carat Gold Price per Gram in Dubai

The 21K gold rate today in UAE is the figure that matters most to Arab households, because 21 carat gold is what Gulf jewellery has been made from for generations. Walk through the Central Souk in Sharjah, the gold rows of Al Ain or the Arabic-design counters in Deira and the heavy sets, the shabka pieces and the everyday bangles are nearly all stamped 875.

The live card below gives the 21 carat gold price in Dubai per gram in dirhams. The rest of this page explains why this karat exists at all, how it is priced against the other karats, what a typical set costs in metal terms, and where the best 21K selections are in the country.

Quick answer

  • 21K gold is 87.5% pure (875 fineness) and costs 87.5% of the 24K rate per gram.
  • It is the house karat of Gulf and Arab jewellery, so Emirati and Arab buyers see it everywhere.
  • The 21 carat rate is national: one price per gram across all seven emirates.
  • 21K is slightly harder than 22K, which suits daily-wear bangles, rings and wedding sets.

Why 21 carat is the Gulf's gold

Every gold-buying culture settles on a working purity, and the Arab world chose 21K. The reasoning is practical. At 875 fineness a piece keeps a deep yellow colour that reads as "real gold" to the eye, while the extra 4% of alloy compared with 22K adds just enough hardness for the big hollow bangles, wide chokers and coin-studded pieces that Gulf and Levantine designs favour. Those pieces are worn, not kept in boxes, and 21K survives daily wear better than softer karats.

There is also a cost logic. A 21K gram costs 87.5% of the 24K rate, so a large set is noticeably cheaper in metal than the same set in 22K, which matters when a shabka or mahr runs to tens of grams. Families from Egypt, Jordan, Syria, Saudi Arabia and the Emirates know the 21K rate the way others know the 22K rate.

The result in the UAE is a market with two parallel traditions sitting on the same street: Indian 22K on one counter, Arabic 21K on the next. If you are not sure which tradition you belong to, the karat guide hub helps you choose.

How 875 gold is priced against the other karats

The 21K price is not set independently; it falls out of the 24K price by arithmetic. Take the international ounce price in dollars, divide by 31.1035 for a gram, multiply by the fixed 3.6725 dirham peg, and that gives the 24K gram rate. Multiply by 0.875 and you have 21K. The Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group publishes the retail version of these numbers daily and shops copy it onto their boards.

21K versus 22K and 18K: choosing with your eyes open

The three karats serve different jobs, and the choice is rarely about which is "better".

21K or 22K?

If the jewellery will stay in the family or be resold within the Arab community, 21K is the natural choice: more designs, a lower per-gram cost, and a resale market that prices 875 pieces instantly. If it may be sold in India, Pakistan or Sri Lanka one day, 22K is safer because buyers there expect 916 and may discount anything else. The 22K rate page covers that side of the street.

21K or 18K?

18K is the karat of diamond jewellery and Italian or Turkish fashion chains, because the harder alloy holds stones and takes fine finishes. For plain gold that you are buying partly as a store of value, 21K gives you much more metal per dirham. See the 18K rate page for where 750 gold earns its place.

Colour and feel

21K is a shade less saturated than 22K and clearly richer than 18K yellow. Hollow 21K bangles can look large for their weight, which is a feature rather than a trick: you get presence without paying for solid metal, but check the walls are not so thin they dent.

Worked examples: what the 21K rate means for a real purchase

Multiply the rate by the weight, then add the jeweller's charge and VAT. Use the calculator for live figures; the shapes below are what residents actually buy.

  1. A 7 gram 21K bangle for daily wear. Seven times the rate for the metal. Machine-made Gulf bangles carry a low per-gram making charge, so the metal is the overwhelming share of the bill.
  2. A 25 gram 21K necklace and earring set. Twenty-five times the rate. This is where you should ask whether the making charge is per gram or a percentage; on a set this size a percentage charge adds up fast.
  3. A 100 gram 21K shabka. One hundred times the rate, a substantial figure, and the point at which a half-dirham difference in the rate matters less than a few dirhams of making charge per gram. Negotiate the charge, not the rate.

Many Gulf families set the shabka budget as a weight rather than a dirham figure, which quietly protects them from rate swings between engagement and wedding. The making charges page explains how to compare two quotes properly.

Where to find the best 21K selection in the UAE

Arabic 21K designs are strongest where Arab customers shop, which is not always the famous souk.

  • Sharjah's Central (Blue) Souk and the Rolla gold area: probably the deepest 21K selection in the country for Gulf, Egyptian and Levantine styles, with many Arab-run shops where bargaining on making charges is expected. The Sharjah gold souk guide has the details.
  • Deira Gold Souk, the Arabic counters: alongside the Indian 22K stock there is a solid 21K trade, especially in the older alleys near Al Ras.
  • Madinat Zayed, Abu Dhabi: the capital's Arab families shop here, so 21K dominates the windows.
  • Al Ain souk and Ajman souk: smaller, local, and very much 21K territory, with classic designs rather than fashion pieces.
  • Mall jewellers in Dubai and Abu Dhabi: curated 21K collections, fixed making charges and easy exchanges for buyers who prefer not to haggle.
Ask for the weight of the clasp and any coins or pendants separately. On a 21K coin necklace the coins are often a lower or different karat than the chain, and a careful shop will price the two parts on different lines.

Buying, selling and VAT on 21K jewellery

All 21K jewellery in the UAE carries 5% VAT on the full price, metal and making charge included. Tourists can reclaim it on departure through the refund scheme if the piece leaves within 90 days and the receipt is above the minimum. Residents cannot, so the tax is simply part of the cost of wearing gold rather than holding bars. The VAT and refund page has the step-by-step.

When you sell 21K pieces, a buyer applies the 875 fineness to the net weight and deducts a margin. Because Arab designs are so common here, 21K resale is quick and competitive in Sharjah and Deira, and a plain bangle will fetch close to its metal value. Ornate pieces lose their making charge entirely, which is the usual argument for buying simpler designs if you may sell. The selling guide covers how to get several quotes in an afternoon.

If you are weighing 21K jewellery against bars or coins purely as a way to hold wealth, the investing hub sets out the trade-offs without pushing you either way.

Questions people ask

What is 21K gold and is it real gold?

21K gold is an alloy that is 21 parts gold in 24, or 875 parts per thousand, which makes it 87.5% pure. It is absolutely real gold, just mixed with copper and silver for strength and colour. It is the standard purity for Gulf, Egyptian and Levantine jewellery and is stamped 875 or 21K on the inside of the piece.

Why is 21K cheaper than 22K in Dubai?

Only because it contains less gold per gram: 87.5% versus 91.6%. The price of each gram of actual gold inside the two alloys is identical. On the shop board the 21K line will always sit about 4.5% below the 22K line, whatever the market is doing, because the difference is purity rather than any local discount.

Is the 21 carat gold rate different in Sharjah or Abu Dhabi?

No. The UAE uses one published retail rate per karat for all emirates, so the 21K metal price in Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Ajman and Dubai is the same at any moment. Shops differ in making charges, stock and willingness to negotiate, which is why Sharjah often feels cheaper for 21K, but the gram rate itself is national.

Can I sell 21K gold bought in the UAE in India?

You can, but Indian buyers are set up for 22K and will usually value a 21K piece at its 875 metal content with a deduction, or ask to melt it. If resale in India is likely, buying 22K avoids that friction. Within the UAE and across the Arab world, 21K sells easily at the published rate minus a small margin.

Does 21K gold tarnish or fade?

Gold itself does not tarnish, and at 87.5% purity 21K stays bright for decades with ordinary care. The copper in the alloy can dull very slightly with sweat and perfume over years, which a jeweller removes with a quick polish. Store pieces separately so harder items do not scratch softer ones, and avoid chlorine pools while wearing them.
