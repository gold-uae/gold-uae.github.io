Privacy Policy

Gold UAE is a static website. There is no login, no account, no newsletter form and no comment box, so there is very little personal data for us to collect in the first place. This page explains, in plain words, the few ways the site touches data: the server that delivers the pages, the price feeds your browser calls to show live rates, and optional web fonts. If anything here changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.

What the site collects and what it does not

No accounts. You cannot register, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number to read a page. The only time we receive personal information is when you choose to email us, and then we keep your message only as long as needed to reply.

No cookies set by the site. Gold UAE itself does not place cookies, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers in your browser. Settings such as a chosen language live in the address of the page, not in stored data. If a future feature ever needs to remember a preference, it will be stored only in your own browser and described on this page first.

Anonymous server logs. Like every website, the server that delivers our pages records standard technical logs: the page requested, the time, the browser type and a network address. These logs are used only to keep the site running and to count rough traffic, are not combined with any other data, and are deleted on a routine schedule.

Live prices fetched in your browser

The rate boards, calculator and charts on this site show live numbers. To do that, your browser, not our server, requests data directly from two public services while the page is open:

  • api.gold-api.com for the international gold spot price in US dollars;
  • open.er-api.com for currency exchange rates used to convert into dirhams, rupees and other currencies.

When your browser makes those requests, each service sees your network address and a standard browser request, exactly as it would if you visited them directly. We do not send them anything about you, and we do not receive anything about you back. Each provider has its own privacy terms, which apply to that request. If you block these requests, the pages still load but the price widgets show no figures; the guides on karats, buying and souks work without them.

Fonts, links and your rights

Pages may load typefaces from Google Fonts so that English and Arabic text render consistently. That request also shows Google a network address and is governed by Google's own privacy terms. The site is designed to remain readable if font loading is blocked.

Articles link to other pages on this site and occasionally mention external organisations by name without linking. We are not responsible for the practices of any third-party site you reach from here.

Because we hold almost no data, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us and want that message removed, or have any question about this policy, write to contact@gold-uae.com and we will deal with it promptly. The contact page describes what else we can help with, and the disclaimer covers how the information itself should be used.
