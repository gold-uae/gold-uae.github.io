If you want a single number in your head, most people here follow the 22K or 21K gram rate, because that is what their jewellery is made of. Investors and anyone buying bars watch the 24K line instead.

How the Dubai gold rate is set each day

There is no committee that invents a Dubai price. The starting point is the global spot price of gold, quoted in US dollars per troy ounce and traded around the clock between London, New York and Asia. Dividing that ounce price by 31.1035 gives a dollar price per gram.

The dirham has been fixed at AED 3.6725 to the dollar since 1997, so converting to AED is pure arithmetic with no currency risk in the middle. That is why the gold rate in AED moves one-to-one with the dollar spot price: if gold rises 1% in New York overnight, the UAE gram rate opens roughly 1% higher.

The Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group publishes a retail reference rate from that calculation and updates it through the trading day, and shops from Deira to Al Ain display it on their boards. The result is one rate for the whole country; a bangle in Sharjah and the same bangle in Abu Dhabi carry the same metal price. Our page on who sets the Dubai gold rate goes into the mechanics, and the spot price in USD page tracks the ounce figure itself.

What moves the gold price

Because the UAE rate is simply the world price in dirhams, the things that move it are global, not local. The big ones, in rough order of how often they matter:

  • US interest rates. Gold pays no interest, so when US rates rise, holding gold becomes relatively less attractive and the price often softens; rate cuts tend to do the opposite.
  • The US dollar. A weaker dollar usually lifts the dollar price of gold, and since the dirham is pegged, that lift arrives in AED unchanged.
  • Central bank buying. Several central banks have been adding reserves in recent years, which has been a steady source of demand.
  • Geopolitics and fear. Conflict, banking stress or inflation scares push investors into gold as a safe haven, sometimes within hours.
  • Physical demand seasons. Indian wedding months, Diwali, Eid and Chinese New Year lift jewellery buying, which supports prices at the margin.

24K, 22K, 21K or 18K: which rate should you follow?

Karat tells you how much of the alloy is actually gold, measured in twenty-fourths. The fineness stamp inside a piece says the same thing in parts per thousand.

KaratFinenessShare of the 24K rateTypical use in the UAE
24K999100%Bars, coins, some bridal sets for Chinese and Gulf buyers
22K91691.6%Indian, Pakistani and Sri Lankan jewellery; the busiest row on any board
21K87587.5%Emirati, Saudi, Egyptian and Levantine designs
18K75075%Italian chains, diamond settings, white and rose gold

Lower karat is harder and holds fine detail and stones better, which is why 18K dominates diamond jewellery. Higher karat is softer but holds more resale value per gram. If you are buying mainly as a store of value, 24K or 22K keep most of the money in the metal; if you want a ring you will wear daily, 18K or 21K will survive better.

From board rate to shop price: the gold calculator

The number on the board is where the conversation starts, not where it ends. A retail receipt in the UAE is built in three layers:

  1. Metal value: the karat rate per gram multiplied by the weight on the scale.
  2. Making charge: the jeweller's fee for the work, quoted either as dirhams per gram or as a percentage of the metal value. Plain bangles and chains sit at the low end; intricate bridal sets and designer brands at the high end.
  3. VAT at 5% on the total for jewellery. Investment bars and coins of 99% purity or higher are zero-rated.

Gold rate by emirate: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and beyond

The per-gram rate is identical in all seven emirates, yet people search for their own city because the shopping experience differs. Dubai has the deepest market: the Gold Souk in Deira, Meena Bazaar in Bur Dubai, the Karama shops and the Gold & Diamond Park on Sheikh Zayed Road. Abu Dhabi's trade clusters around Madinat Zayed and Hamdan Street; Sharjah's around the Central Souk and Rolla; Ajman, Al Ain, Fujairah and Ras Al Khaimah each have their own smaller gold rows.

Jewellery and investing: two different ways to own gold

Most gold in UAE homes is jewellery, and the jewellery section is organised by what you are shopping for: rings, chains, necklaces and sets, bangles and bracelets and earrings, each with typical weights so the calculator gives you a sensible budget.

Investing is a different mindset. Here the making charge is the enemy and purity is everything, which is why serious savers buy bars and coins at a small premium over the 24K rate, or skip physical metal altogether through online trading accounts and gold ETFs and mining stocks. Questions people ask

Is the gold rate the same in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah?

Yes. The metal rate per gram is a national figure derived from the dollar spot price, and shops in every emirate display the same board. What differs between cities and between shops is the making charge, the discount a particular jeweller will give, and the range of designs on offer. Compare those rather than hunting for a cheaper gram rate, because there is not one.

Why is the gold rate quoted per gram in the UAE and per ounce internationally?

Wholesale markets trade in troy ounces of 31.1035 grams because that is the London convention. Retail in the Gulf and South Asia works in grams (and tolas of 11.6638 grams) because jewellery is weighed in grams on the counter. The two are the same price in different units; our board already does the ounce-to-gram and dollar-to-dirham conversion for you.

Does the gold rate change during the day?

It does. The spot price trades almost continuously from Monday morning in Asia to Friday evening in New York, so the UAE gram rate drifts throughout the working day and shops update their boards a few times daily. Over a weekend the rate freezes at Friday's close and reopens with a gap on Monday if news has moved the market.

What is the best time to buy gold in Dubai?

Nobody can time the metal price reliably, and we do not try. What you can time is the making charge: festival periods such as the Dubai Shopping Festival, Eid and Diwali bring promotions on making charges, and quieter summer weeks give you more room to negotiate. Buying plain, high-karat pieces also keeps more of your money in the metal regardless of the season.

How much VAT do I pay on gold in the UAE?

Jewellery carries 5% VAT on the full invoice, metal and making charge included. Investment-grade gold of 99% purity or higher, such as bars and most coins, is zero-rated. Tourists can reclaim the VAT paid on jewellery through the Planet refund desks at the airport when leaving, provided the purchase meets the minimum and the goods leave the country within ninety days.

Can I check today's gold rate in Indian or Pakistani rupees here?

Yes. The currency pages convert the live UAE gram rate into Indian, Pakistani, Nepali and Sri Lankan rupees as well as Bangladeshi taka, Philippine pesos and Egyptian pounds, using a live exchange rate. They also summarise what you may carry home duty-free, which matters more than the price difference for most travellers.
