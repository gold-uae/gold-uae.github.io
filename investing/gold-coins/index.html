Gold Coin Price in Dubai: 1 g, 8 g, Sovereign-Size and Bullion Coins Compared

Gold coins sit between bars and jewellery. They are small enough to gift at a wedding or an Eid gathering, pure enough to count as investment gold when they are 24K, and recognisable enough to sell in any gold shop from Deira to Al Ain. The catch is that "gold coin" in Dubai covers three quite different products: international bullion coins, traditional 21K and 22K coins used across the Gulf and South Asia, and tiny gift coins stamped by jewellers. They are priced differently, taxed differently and resold differently. This page separates them so the gold coin price you are quoted makes sense.

Quick answer

  • A gold coin in Dubai is priced on its gold content at the karat rate plus a premium for minting and design.
  • Bullion coins are 24K and VAT-free; traditional 21K and 22K coins are jewellery for tax purposes.
  • 1 g and 8 g gift coins carry the highest premium per gram; 1 oz bullion coins the lowest.
  • Check the fine gold weight, not the gross weight, before comparing prices.
  • Coins resell at any UAE gold shop, with a better price for sealed, recognised designs.

The first group is what an investor means by coins. The second is what an Arab or South Asian family means: a coin that can be worn on a chain or sewn onto a bridal outfit and later sold by weight. The third is the gifting market that peaks before Diwali, Eid and Dubai Shopping Festival. Keep the three apart and the prices stop looking inconsistent.

How a gold coin price is calculated in Dubai

Every coin follows the same formula as a bar: fine gold weight multiplied by the day's rate for that purity, plus a premium. The trap is the first term. A traditional 21K coin weighing 7 g contains 7 × 0.875 = 6.125 g of pure gold, so it should be priced on the 21K rate, not the 24K one. A 1 oz bullion coin in 22K alloy contains a full troy ounce of fine gold and weighs more than 31.1 g in total; its price follows the 24K rate for the fine content.

The premium covers minting, the mint's own margin, packaging and the seller's margin. It is largest on tiny coins and on anything with a popular design, and smallest on plain 1 oz bullion. Design premiums are real money: a coin stamped with a well-loved motif will sell for more than a plain one of the same weight, and may also buy back for more at the same shop, but rarely at a different one.

1 gram and 8 gram gold coins: the gift sizes

Two sizes dominate searches for coins in Dubai, and both are gifting products rather than investment ones.

The 1 gram gold coin

Sold in a sealed card by nearly every jeweller, usually 999.9, often stamped with a brand or a festive motif. The premium per gram is the highest of anything on this page because the mint cost is spread over a single gram. It is a lovely present for a newborn or a graduate and a poor way to accumulate gold; ten 1 g coins cost noticeably more than one 10 g bar. If you are buying several, consider a 5 g or 10 g coin or a bar from the gold bars page.

The 8 gram gold coin

Eight grams is the classic Indian coin weight, descended from the old sovereign tradition, and remains the standard wedding and Dhanteras gift among Indian residents in Bur Dubai, Karama and Meena Bazaar. It comes in 22K with traditional designs or 24K as a plain bullion-style coin. The 22K version is jewellery for VAT purposes; the 24K version may qualify as investment gold if it is a tradable bullion form. The premium is far lower per gram than on 1 g coins, which makes 8 g a sensible middle ground for gifts that are also meant to hold value.

Between them sit 2 g, 4 g and 5 g coins, mostly 22K, sold by the same shops for the same occasions.

Traditional 21K and 22K coins in the Gulf

Long before bullion coins reached Dubai, families in the Gulf, Egypt, the Levant and South Asia stored wealth in gold coins they could also wear. That market is still alive in the souks and it works on its own logic.

  • Lira-style and guinea-style coins in 21K are stocked by Deira and Sharjah shops for Egyptian, Syrian, Jordanian and Lebanese customers who know them from home. They are sold by weight at the 21K rate plus a modest making charge and strung on chains or bracelets. The 21K page explains why this karat dominates Arab demand.
  • Indian-style 22K coins with temple, deity or Lakshmi designs are the Diwali staple, priced at the 22K rate plus a charge, and often given in sets of five or eleven.
  • Coin jewellery, meaning coins set into bangles, pendants and belts, is priced as jewellery with full making charges, which it is.

These coins are liquid in the UAE because any shop can weigh and test them, but they are bought back at metal value, so the making charge and any design premium are not recovered. They also attract 5% VAT at purchase. For a family that will wear the coins and keep them for decades, that is a fair trade; for a pure investor, a 24K bullion coin or bar is cheaper to hold.

Bullion coins versus bars: which to choose

If your goal is investment rather than gifting, the real comparison is between a 24K coin and a 24K bar of similar total weight.

  • Premium: a 1 oz bullion coin usually costs a little more than a 1 oz or 31 g bar from the same dealer. Below 1 oz the gap widens; a 1/10 oz coin is expensive per gram.
  • Divisibility: four 1/4 oz coins can be sold one at a time; a 1 oz bar cannot. That flexibility is what you pay the premium for.
  • Recognition: major bullion coins are known at every dealer worldwide and need no assay card. Bars rely on their packaging; a bar in a damaged pack gets tested.
  • Durability: coins get scratched and handled; a scratched bullion coin still sells by weight but loses any collector premium. Bars stay sealed.
  • Storage: coins take more space per gram and tubes or capsules are worth buying.

A common pattern among residents who save steadily is a core of 10 g, 20 g or 50 g bars bought when a bonus lands, with a few 1/4 oz or 8 g coins added for gifting and for selling in small lots. The investing hub sets that choice beside online accounts and funds for readers who want no metal at all.

Before paying for any coin, ask the seller to state the fine gold weight in grams on the invoice. A "7 gram coin" at 21K is 6.125 g of gold, and a "1 ounce coin" in 22K alloy weighs about 34 g in total. Comparing fine weight against the live rate is the only way to see the true premium.

Where to buy gold coins in Dubai and the UAE

Coins are stocked more widely than bars, but the right counter depends on the type you want.

  1. Bullion desks near the Deira Gold Souk and in the Gold & Diamond Park: international 1 oz and fractional coins, tubes of coins for bulk buyers, and the tightest premiums. Several also sell locally minted 24K coins in capsules.
  2. Mall branches of the big jewellery chains: the widest choice of 1 g to 10 g gift coins in branded packs, with published premiums and buyback at any branch. Busy and well stocked before Diwali and the Eids.
  3. Souk shops in Deira, Meena Bazaar and Karama: 22K Indian-style coins and sets, often with lower making charges than the malls and more room to negotiate on larger quantities.
  4. Sharjah's Rolla gold area and the Central Souk, and Abu Dhabi's Madinat Zayed Gold Centre: the best place for 21K lira-style and guinea-style coins and coin jewellery.
  5. Banks and exchange houses: a few sell 24K coins and small bars at a counter or via an app, with a bank receipt.

Online ordering from UAE jewellers is now routine for gift coins, with delivery across the emirates; stick to sellers with a physical branch so you have somewhere to sell back. The jewellers and brands page describes who specialises in what, and the Gold & Diamond Park guide covers the Sheikh Zayed Road option.

VAT, invoices and carrying coins abroad

Tax treatment follows the product, not the word "coin". A 24K coin in a recognised bullion form is zero-rated for VAT, like a bar. A 22K or 21K coin is jewellery and carries 5%, which tourists can reclaim through the Planet refund scheme at departure if the single purchase is at least AED 250 and the goods leave within 90 days. An ornamental 24K coin in a decorative frame may be taxed at some sellers; ask before paying.

Whichever type you buy, insist on a tax invoice that lists the purity, the gross weight, the fine weight where relevant and any serial number, and keep the original packaging. Those two things set the buyback price later. Taking coins out of the UAE is simple on the Dubai side and complicated on the arrival side: India, for example, gives returning residents a duty-free allowance for worn jewellery, but treats coins as dutiable gold. The customs page explains the allowances and the current duty, and the VAT page covers the refund procedure at the airport.

Selling gold coins back in the UAE

Selling a coin is quick in the UAE. The shop checks the design, weighs it, confirms the purity with an XRF reading or an acid test on traditional coins, and pays the day's buyback rate for the fine content, less a small margin. What varies is how much of the premium you recover.

  • Major bullion coins in capsules sell back close to spot at bullion desks.
  • Branded gift coins sell best at the chain that sold them, which may honour part of its own premium; elsewhere they are weighed as 24K metal.
  • Traditional 21K and 22K coins are bought at metal weight only, like any jewellery, with the making charge lost.
  • Damaged, drilled or mounted coins are weighed as scrap of their karat.

Get two quotes on the same day, one from a bullion desk and one from a jeweller, and bring the original invoice. If you only need cash briefly, pledging the coins for a gold loan may cost less than selling and buying again. Questions people ask

How much is a 1 gram gold coin in Dubai?

The metal value is one gram at the live 24K rate shown in the strip above; the shop then adds a premium for minting, the card and its margin, which on a 1 g coin is a large share of the total. Prices differ between chains mainly on that premium. A 1 g coin is a gift product; for saving, a 5 g or 10 g coin or bar costs much less per gram.

What is the price of an 8 gram gold coin in Dubai?

An 8 g coin in 22K contains about 7.33 g of fine gold and is priced on the 22K rate plus a making charge and 5% VAT; an 8 g coin in 24K is priced on the 24K rate plus a smaller premium and may be VAT-free if it is a tradable bullion form. Ask which you are being offered, then check the live 22K or 24K rate and add the quoted premium.

Are gold coins VAT-free in the UAE?

Only when they are investment gold: 99% purity or higher in a tradable bullion form. Traditional 21K and 22K coins are jewellery and carry 5% VAT, which tourists can reclaim at departure. Some ornamental 24K gift coins in decorative packaging are also charged VAT by certain sellers. The invoice will show the VAT line, so check it before paying.

Which is better for investment, gold coins or gold bars?

Bars are cheaper per gram, especially above 50 g, so they suit building a holding. Coins cost a little more but can be sold or gifted one at a time and are recognised without an assay card. Many residents keep bars as the core and a few coins for flexibility. For gifting, a coin almost always beats a bar in how it is received.

Where is the best place to buy gold coins in Dubai?

For bullion coins, the dealers around the Deira Gold Souk and in the Gold & Diamond Park offer the lowest premiums. For branded gift coins with easy exchange, the mall branches of the major chains. For 22K Indian-style coins, Meena Bazaar and Karama; for 21K lira-style coins, Deira, Sharjah Rolla and Abu Dhabi's Madinat Zayed. Compare the premium, since the rate is the same everywhere.
