Gold Bangles and Bracelets in Dubai: Prices, Weights and Styles for Women and Men

Ask a Gulf grandmother or a Keralite aunt what gold to buy and the answer is usually the same: bangles. They are heavy, plain, easy to weigh, easy to sell and carry the smallest making charge in the shop. A set of bangles bought at a wedding often becomes the family's emergency fund decades later. This guide covers the gold bangles price in Dubai and how it is built, the weights you can expect per bangle and per set, the main styles across Gulf, Indian and Western tastes, men's gold bracelets, gold anklets, and the sizing question that trips up online buyers.

Quick answer

  • Gold bangles in Dubai are sold by weight: grams times the karat rate, plus a low per-gram making charge and 5% VAT.
  • Plain bangles have the lowest making charge of any jewellery, which makes them the classic savings piece.
  • A single 22K bangle usually weighs 8 to 20 grams; a bridal set of four or six can pass 100 grams.
  • Bangle size is measured by inner diameter; bring an existing bangle or get your hand measured in the shop.
  • Men's gold bracelets are mostly 18K or 21K curb and Cuban links of 15 to 40 grams.

Gold bangles price in Dubai: why they are the cleanest purchase

A bangle is a ring of metal. Put it on the scale, multiply the grams by today's rate for its karat, add a making charge that for plain designs is a small per-gram figure, add 5% VAT, and you have the price. There is no clasp, no chain to kink, often no stones, and the weight is large enough that the making charge is a small share of the total.

Compare that with a ring, where the making charge may rival the metal, or a necklace set, where hand-work can add a large premium. On a pair of plain 22K bangles weighing 30 grams together, the making charge is often a minor line and the metal is almost everything. That is why bangles are the piece residents buy when they want jewellery that behaves like savings, and why the making charges page uses bangles as the baseline.

Price any weight at today's figure on the calculator, then compare the making charge per gram between shops, since the metal part never changes from counter to counter.

Bangle weights: single, pair and bridal sets

Bangle weight depends on width, thickness, whether the bangle is solid or hollow, and the size. The ranges below are typical on UAE shop scales.

PieceUsual weightCommon karat
Thin daily-wear bangle (kada or churi)6 to 10 g each22K or 21K
Medium plain bangle10 to 18 g each22K or 21K
Broad or patterned bangle18 to 35 g each22K, 21K
Bridal set of four or six60 to 150 g total22K
Gulf-style wide cuff25 to 60 g21K

Bangles are sold singly, in pairs or in sets of four, six or twelve, and the making charge per gram tends to fall as the set grows. A pair of medium 22K bangles around 25 to 30 grams is the most common wedding gift in the Indian community of the UAE; a set of six is a common bridal purchase.

Hollow bangles exist and are popular in broad designs because they give size without weight. They are legitimate but dent easily and resell only for their actual metal, so confirm "solid or hollow" and read the invoice.

Styles: Gulf, Indian and Western bangles

Three traditions sit side by side in the souk, and knowing the vocabulary helps you ask for what you want.

Gulf and Arab

21K wide cuffs and hinged bangles with raised geometric or floral work, often sold as a pair for the two wrists; Bahraini and Emirati designs with hanging coins; and modern Italian-influenced 21K pieces in Deira and Sharjah's Rolla.

Indian and Pakistani

22K rules. Plain round churis sold by the dozen, broad kadas with engraved or filigree work, Kerala-style plain bangles with a tiny motif, Punjabi kadas for men and women, and bridal sets with meenakari colour. Bur Dubai and Karama are the centres, and stock peaks before Diwali, Onam and the wedding season.

Western and branded

18K tennis bracelets, bangles with diamonds or coloured stones, and slim stackable 18K bangles in yellow, white and rose gold from the mall jewellers and the Gold and Diamond Park.

Within all three, the plain polished bangle is the value buy and the worked bangle is the fashion buy. Our 22K and 21K rate pages show today's per-gram difference.

Gold bracelets for women

A bracelet differs from a bangle in having a clasp and flexible links, so it fits a wider range of wrists and is easier to put on alone. Popular gold bracelet for women styles in the UAE include thin 18K and 21K chain bracelets with a small charm, slightly heavier 21K and 22K link bracelets of 5 to 15 grams, and stone-set 18K tennis bracelets. Charm bracelets in 21K with hanging hearts, evil-eye motifs or names are a staple gift for Eid and graduations.

Because a bracelet has a clasp and more complex links than a bangle, the making charge per gram is higher, and on a very light bracelet the charge can be a sizeable share of the price. Lobster clasps and a safety chain are worth the small extra for anything above a few grams. Bracelets with hollow links look substantial but weigh little; press a link gently to check.

For resale, a plain link bracelet in 21K or 22K behaves much like a chain, returning most of its metal value, while a stone-set 18K piece returns only the 18K gold. See our chain guide for how link construction affects durability.

Gold bracelets for men

Men's gold bracelets have become a serious category in the UAE, especially among Gulf and African buyers. The common styles are Cuban and curb links in 21K, Figaro and rope in 18K Italian make, and solid 22K kadas for South Asian men. Weights run from 15 grams for a slim bracelet to 40 grams and beyond for a heavy Cuban, with the men's counters in Deira and Sharjah carrying the heaviest stock.

Three things to check. Length: a men's bracelet usually sits at 19 to 22 cm; measure the wrist and add a finger's width for comfort. Construction: heavy-looking men's bracelets are often hollow, and a hollow Cuban dents on a door frame and cannot be repaired cleanly; for a bracelet worn daily, solid links are worth the weight. Clasp: a box clasp with a safety catch holds better than a lobster on anything above 20 grams.

On price, a plain solid men's bracelet is close to a chain in making charge terms, so it is one of the better value-per-gram choices for a man who wants gold he can wear and later sell. The 18K page shows today's rate for Italian styles.

For a bangle that will never come off, choose a hinged or screw-type design that opens; a closed bangle must slide over the knuckles, and hands change size with age, heat and pregnancy.

Gold anklets and children's bangles

Gold anklets are a traditional purchase in South Asian and Arab families and are sold in 21K and 22K, usually 5 to 15 grams each and often in pairs. They take more knocks than any other piece, so a sturdier link and a secure clasp matter more than design; many shops suggest 21K over 22K for the extra hardness. Anklets with bells are heavier than they look because the bells are solid.

Children's bangles are a UAE souk staple: tiny 22K or 21K pairs of 2 to 6 grams each, often with a screw or hinge, bought for naming ceremonies, first birthdays and Eid. They are priced on weight like adult bangles but carry a higher per-gram making charge because the work is fine and the weight small. Buy slightly large and have the bangle resized, or choose an adjustable design; children's wrists grow fast.

Both anklets and children's pieces should be sized in the shop. A UAE goldsmith can adjust most bangles by a few millimetres while you wait, and many shops will exchange a children's bangle for a larger size at the metal rate plus a small charge as the child grows.

Sizing a bangle: the number that matters

Bangle size is the inner diameter, usually quoted in inches by Indian shops (2.4, 2.6, 2.8 and so on) and in millimetres by Gulf and Western shops. A closed bangle must pass over the knuckles, so the hand is measured at its widest point with the thumb tucked in. The shop will try several sizers on you; if buying online, measure an existing bangle that fits with a ruler across the inside.

  1. Fold the thumb into the palm and squeeze the hand as if putting on a bangle.
  2. Measure the circumference around the widest part of the knuckles with a strip of paper.
  3. Divide by 3.14 to get the diameter and compare with the shop's size chart.
  4. If between sizes, go up for a closed bangle and down for a hinged one.

Hinged, screw and openable bangles solve the knuckle problem and suit older buyers and anyone with arthritis. Resizing a closed plain bangle is routine in the souk; a patterned or stone-set bangle is harder and may show the join, so get the size right first.

Where to buy and how to sell later

For 22K bangles in Indian and Pakistani styles, the Meena Bazaar lanes in Bur Dubai and the shops of Karama hold the deepest stock and compete hard on making charges. The Deira Gold Souk carries the widest 21K Gulf selection alongside 22K, and the Gold and Diamond Park is the place for 18K stone-set bracelets and custom work. In Abu Dhabi the Madinat Zayed Gold Centre is the equivalent; Sharjah's Rolla area is strong for 21K cuffs; Ajman's souk is small but keen.

Selling a bangle is as simple as buying one: the shop weighs it, tests the karat and pays the metal value minus a modest deduction. Plain bangles bought with a low making charge return a very high share of what you paid for the metal, which is the whole point of buying them. With the original invoice, most shops will exchange at a better rate than a cash sale. Our selling gold guide and the gold loan page explain the options when the bangles need to work for you.

Questions people ask

How much do gold bangles cost in Dubai?

Weigh the bangle, multiply by today's per-gram rate for its karat, add a low per-gram making charge and 5% VAT. A single medium 22K bangle is usually 10 to 18 grams. Since the rate is the same in every UAE shop, compare only the making charge per gram between counters. The live strip on this page gives the current rate.

Are gold bangles a good way to save?

Many UAE families treat plain bangles exactly that way. The making charge is the lowest of any jewellery, the weight is easy to verify, and buy-back is straightforward at the metal rate. The trade-off is that you still pay VAT and a small making charge that are not returned, so a bar or coin is more efficient if you never intend to wear the gold.

What is the best karat for bangles worn daily?

21K for Gulf buyers and 22K for South Asian buyers are the traditional choices and both are fine for bangles, because a bangle is thick enough to resist bending. Thin bangles and anklets are better in 21K or 18K for the extra hardness. Very wide hollow bangles dent in any karat, so prefer solid construction for daily wear.

How do I know my bangle size?

Tuck your thumb into your palm, measure around the widest part of your knuckles with a paper strip, and divide by 3.14 to get the inner diameter. Indian shops quote sizes in inches such as 2.4 or 2.6; Gulf and Western shops use millimetres. If you are between sizes, choose the larger for a closed bangle.

Are men's gold bracelets cheaper per gram than women's?

Often slightly, because men's bracelets are heavier and plainer, so the making charge per gram is lower. A solid 21K Cuban link of 30 grams is priced close to a chain of the same weight. Watch for hollow construction, which is common in heavy-looking men's pieces and lowers both durability and resale.
