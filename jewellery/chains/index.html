Gold Chain Price in Dubai: 22K, 18K, Men's and Ladies' Chains by Weight

If you want to understand how gold is priced in the UAE, buy a chain. No other piece shows the arithmetic so plainly: the chain goes on the scale, the weight is multiplied by the board rate for its karat, a small per-gram making charge is added, then VAT. Because chains are produced in long runs on machines, that making charge is usually the lowest in the shop, which is why residents who want jewellery that also holds value so often choose a plain chain. This page covers gold chain price in Dubai by weight, the difference between 22K, 21K and 18K chains, the link styles for men and women, and the hollow-versus-solid question that catches many buyers.

Quick answer

  • A gold chain in Dubai is priced by weight: grams times the karat rate, plus a per-gram making charge and 5% VAT.
  • Chains carry the lowest making charges in the shop because most are machine-made in long runs.
  • A 10 gram chain costs the same in metal everywhere in the UAE; only the karat and making charge change the total.
  • 22K chains are the South Asian standard; 21K suits Gulf buyers; 18K Italian chains are lighter and tougher.
  • Hollow chains look thick but weigh little, so always ask if a chain is solid or hollow before comparing prices.

Gold chain rate in Dubai: the formula and a worked example

Suppose you are looking at a 22K rope chain and the scale reads 12.6 grams. The metal value is 12.6 multiplied by today's 22K per-gram rate, the figure on the digital board by the door and on our live strip. The shop adds its making charge, quoted per gram for chains, which falls as weight rises because the labour per gram is lower on a longer, heavier chain. Then 5% VAT goes on the total. That is the whole price; there is no hidden line.

Two implications follow. First, the metal part of a 12.6 gram chain is identical in Deira, Sharjah, Ajman and Al Ain on the same day, so comparing "chain prices" between shops really means comparing making charges for similar designs. Second, when the rate moves during the day the shop re-prices the chain instantly, so if you are buying several grams, agree the rate and pay rather than wandering off to think.

Run your target weight through the gold calculator for each karat to see how much the karat choice alone moves the bill before you worry about design.

10 gram gold chain price: why quotes differ so much

"How much is a 10 gram gold chain in Dubai?" is one of the most common questions at our desk, and the honest answer is: which 10 grams? Three chains of identical weight can carry very different totals.

22K, 21K or 18K gold chain: which karat to choose

The karat sets both the metal value and how the chain behaves around your neck.

  • 22K gold chain: the default for Indian, Pakistani, Sri Lankan and Bangladeshi buyers in the UAE and the karat most chains in Bur Dubai and Karama are made in. Deep yellow, highest resale value per gram, but the links are soft; a thin 22K chain can stretch or kink if pulled.
  • 21K gold chain: the Gulf and Arab standard, filling the windows of Deira and Sharjah's Rolla. A touch harder than 22K and cheaper per gram, with the same warm colour. Good middle ground for everyday wear.
  • 18K gold chain: Italian and Turkish imports, mall brands and most white or rose gold chains. Hardest and most resistant to kinks, lighter in colour, lowest metal value per gram. The right choice for a thin chain worn daily under a shirt or with a pendant that swings.

A practical rule many souk sellers use: below about 5 grams, go 18K or 21K so the chain lasts; above 15 grams, 22K because the metal is what you are paying for and the thicker links do not kink. Our karat guide compares all four purities side by side.

Gold chains for men: links, weights and lengths

Men's gold chains in the UAE run heavier and plainer than women's. Popular gold chain designs for men are the rope (twisted strands, very strong, hides scratches), the Cuban or curb (flat interlocking links, the Gulf favourite in 21K), the Figaro (alternating long and short links, common in 18K Italian imports), and the box or Franco (square links, good for carrying a pendant). Weights range from a light 8 gram daily chain to 40 or 60 gram statement pieces in the men's shops of Deira and Sharjah.

Length matters more than most men expect. A chain that sits at the collarbone is usually around 45 to 50 cm; one that falls to the sternum, around 55 to 60 cm. Longer chains weigh more at the same thickness, so decide length first, then thickness, then weight.

Men's chains are where hollow construction is most common. A hollow Cuban link looks like a 40 gram chain and weighs 15. It is cheaper for its appearance but dents easily and cannot be repaired cleanly once crushed. Ask the seller directly: solid or hollow? Reputable shops will say which without being asked twice.

Ladies' gold chains and chains for pendants

A gold chain for women in the UAE is usually bought for one of two jobs: to wear alone, thin and close to the neck, or to carry a pendant. For the first, Singapore-style twisted chains, wheat chains and fine box chains in 18K or 21K are the standard, typically 2 to 6 grams. For a pendant, the chain must be strong enough at the bail; a cable or box chain in 21K or 22K between 4 and 10 grams is the common choice, especially for the small religious pendants and name pendants that sell steadily in Deira and Meena Bazaar.

Many ladies' chains are sold with a matching pendant as a "chain set", priced on combined weight with one making charge. If the pendant is stone-set, check whether the stone weight is included in the gold weight on the invoice. A clear invoice protects your resale value.

Chains with lobster clasps hold better than spring-ring clasps on anything above a few grams; ask the shop to swap the clasp if you are buying a heavier chain for daily wear. Pendants and matching sets are covered in our necklaces and sets guide.

Hollow or solid: the question that decides value

Hollow chains are made from tubular gold wire, so each link is a shell. They give a thick look at a fraction of the weight, and in 18K Italian styles they are a legitimate, well-made product. The problem comes when a buyer compares a hollow chain and a solid chain by appearance and assumes the thicker-looking one contains more gold.

Three checks. Weigh both: the scale never lies about gold content. Press a link gently between finger and thumb: a hollow link flexes; a solid one does not. Look at the invoice wording: many shops print "hollow" or "H" next to the description.

Resale is where the difference bites. A buyer pays for metal weight at the karat rate, so a 15 gram hollow chain that cost nearly as much as a 15 gram solid chain because of higher making charges returns only its 15 grams. Dents in hollow links also lower what a shop will offer. For a chain intended to hold value, solid is the safe choice; for a fashion piece worn occasionally, hollow is fine.

Carry a small pocket scale or ask to see the shop scale's reading yourself, then multiply by the board rate on your phone. If the metal value you calculate and the shop's quoted metal value differ, ask why before you discuss the making charge.

Where to buy a gold chain in Dubai and the UAE

For sheer range in 21K and 22K, the Gold Souk in Deira and its covered extension are where chains hang by the hundred and making charges are at their keenest; several shops on the lanes behind the main arcade deal in chains and nothing else. Bur Dubai's Meena Bazaar and the Karama shops carry 22K machine chains in the South Indian and Pakistani styles. Italian 18K chains are strongest at the Gold and Diamond Park and the branded mall stores.

Abu Dhabi buyers head to the Madinat Zayed Gold Centre, where chain prices track Dubai's rate exactly; Sharjah's Central Souk and Rolla are known for 21K men's chains; Ajman's souk is small but friendly on making charges. The gold shops near me page maps each area.

Online, the big UAE jewellers sell chains by weight with the rate locked at checkout, which suits residents who know exactly the gram and style they want. Whichever route you choose, the making charges guide tells you what is reasonable for a chain so you can negotiate from knowledge rather than hope.

Caring for a gold chain and selling it later

Chains collect sweat, perfume and sunscreen in the Gulf climate and dull quickly. Rinse in warm water with a drop of dish soap, brush gently with a soft toothbrush and dry fully before storing; keep each chain in its own pouch so it does not knot with others. Take chains off before the pool and the sea, since chlorine and salt attack the solder at the clasp first.

Repairs are cheap and quick in the souk: a broken link or a worn clasp is usually fixed while you wait. Kinked thin 22K chains can be straightened but rarely look perfect again, which is one more argument for 18K or 21K on very fine chains.

When you sell, the shop weighs the chain, tests the karat and pays metal value minus a deduction. A plain solid chain bought with a low making charge is one of the best pieces to resell, often returning a very high share of what you paid for the metal. Keep the original invoice; with it, exchange against a new piece is usually on better terms than a straight cash sale, as our selling gold page explains.

Questions people ask

How much is a 10 gram gold chain in Dubai?

Multiply 10 by today's per-gram rate for the chain's karat, add the shop's per-gram making charge and 5% VAT. A 22K chain costs more in metal than an 18K one of the same weight. The live strip on this page shows the current per-gram rate, and the calculator gives the metal value instantly.

Is a 22K gold chain better than an 18K chain?

It depends on the job. 22K holds more gold per gram and resells for more, but its soft links stretch and kink on thin chains. 18K is tougher, lighter in colour and cheaper per gram, which suits fine daily-wear chains and pendant chains. For a heavy chain bought partly as savings, 22K; for a thin everyday chain, 18K or 21K.

Why do two chains of the same weight have different prices?

Either the karat differs, so the metal value differs, or the making charge differs because one design is finer, hollow, imported or hand-finished. Within the UAE the per-gram rate for a given karat is the same everywhere on the same day, so any gap between two same-karat, same-weight chains is entirely workmanship and the shop's margin.

Are gold chains cheaper in Dubai than in India?

Usually, for the same karat and weight. The metal costs the same worldwide, but the UAE charges only 5% VAT, which tourists can reclaim, has no import duty on gold and has intense competition on making charges. Check India's customs allowance before buying a heavy chain to carry home; our customs guide covers the limits.

What is the best gold chain design for men in the UAE?

Rope and Cuban links are the most popular because they are strong, hide scratches and sit well at 20 grams and above. Figaro and box chains suit lighter weights and pendants. Gulf buyers lean to 21K Cuban; South Asian buyers to 22K rope. Choose solid over hollow if the chain is meant to keep its value.
