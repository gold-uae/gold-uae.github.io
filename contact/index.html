Contact Gold UAE

The simplest way to reach us is by email at contact@gold-uae.com. We read every message, and corrections get priority: if a rate looks wrong, a souk has changed its hours or a customs rule has been updated, tell us and we will check and fix it. There is no office you can visit and no phone line, because Gold UAE is a small publishing site, not a shop.

What we can help with

We are happy to answer messages about the content of the site and the gold trade in general. Typical emails we respond to:

  • A factual correction to any page, with a link to the page and what you believe is wrong.
  • A question about how a figure on the site is calculated, for example why the 22K rate is a fixed fraction of the 24K rate.
  • A suggestion for a topic we have not covered, such as a souk in your emirate that deserves a page.
  • A report of a broken widget, a chart that will not load or a layout problem on your phone.
  • Feedback on the Arabic pages from native readers, which we especially value.

If your question is already answered on the site we will usually reply with a link, so a quick look at the buying guide or the tools section first can save you a day.

What we do not answer

Some requests arrive every week and we have to decline them, not out of unfriendliness but because an honest answer is impossible or would be irresponsible.

  1. Price predictions. We will not tell you whether gold will rise or fall next week, next month or by Eid. Nobody knows, and our price analysis page explains why.
  2. Valuations from a photo. We cannot tell you what your chain, bangle or coin is worth from a picture. Purity and weight need a scale and a test; any gold shop in Deira, Sharjah or Abu Dhabi will do it in minutes, often for free.
  3. Buy or sell orders. We do not trade gold and cannot connect you with a buyer or seller.
  4. Disputes with a shop. We cannot mediate; the relevant consumer protection authority in your emirate can.
  5. Personal financial advice. Whether you should put your savings into gold depends on your situation, and that is a licensed adviser's job.

Messages of this kind may not receive a reply. Everything else usually does within a few days.

Before you write

Please do not send photographs of identity documents, bank details or receipts with personal information. We do not need them and we would rather not hold them. For a correction, the page address and a sentence or two is enough. For a technical problem, the name of your browser and whether you are on a phone or a computer helps us reproduce it. Our privacy policy explains how the little data the site touches is handled.
