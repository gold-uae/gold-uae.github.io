Rates and tools are indicative

The per-gram rates, the calculator, the charts and the currency conversions are produced in your browser from third-party price and exchange-rate feeds. We do not control those feeds, and they can be delayed, briefly unavailable or occasionally wrong. Our karat figures are arithmetic from the spot price and purity; they are not the official rate of the Dubai Gold and Jewellery Group, of any shop or of any bank.

The price you pay or receive in a shop will differ from any number shown here, because retailers add making charges, VAT on jewellery, and a buying or selling margin, and because they update their boards on their own schedule. Treat our figures as a reference for comparison, never as a quotation. Always confirm the live board rate and the total price, in writing, before you buy or sell. The page on who sets the Dubai rate explains the difference between the international price and the shop board.

Not financial, legal or tax advice

We are not licensed financial advisers, brokers, lawyers or tax consultants, and this site does not create a professional relationship with you. Pages on investing, trading, ETFs, gold loans and price analysis explain how products work and what many market participants do; they do not say what you should do. Gold can fall in value, leveraged products can lose more than you deposit, and past price history is no guide to the future. Make decisions, particularly large ones, with a licensed professional who knows your circumstances.

Information about VAT, tourist refunds, Indian customs allowances and other regulations was believed correct when written, but rules change and may be applied differently in individual cases. Before relying on any of it, check the current notice from the relevant authority, such as the UAE Federal Tax Authority or the Indian customs service, or ask the airport or shop directly.
